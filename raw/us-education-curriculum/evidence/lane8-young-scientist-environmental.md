## 8.1 Young Scientist / research-project competitions (Pillar 1 STEM depth + project methodology)

| Competition | Organizer / official page | Eligibility (official) | Format / rubric signal | Meridian tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3M Young Scientist Challenge** | 3M + Discovery Education - official rules PDF https://youngscientistlab.com/pdfs/2_annual-challenge/2026-Official-Rules-3M-Young-Scientist-Challenge.pdf; program https://www.discoveryeducation.com (2026 cycle) | Enrolled in grades 5-8, at least 10 years old at entry deadline [High - official narrow-fact] | 1-2 min entry video proposing a science-based solution to an everyday problem; 10 finalists + mentorship with a 3M scientist; up to 51 state merit winners; $25,000 grand prize "America's Top Young Scientist" | T1 tail (Gr 5) + T2 (Gr 6-8) project ceiling |

| **Regeneron ISEF** | Society for Science - https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/ and FAQ https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/faq/ | Grades 9-12 or equivalent, must qualify via a Society-affiliated fair; 22 categories [High - official narrow-fact] | Independent research project, max 12 months continuous research; judged by PhD-level scientists; International Rules for Pre-College Science Research (societyforscience.org/ISEF) | T3 (Gr 9-12) research ceiling |
