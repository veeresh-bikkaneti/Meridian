## 1. T2 CALIBRATION RULINGS

**Overall dial for T2 [Inferred]:** Relational / quantitative-lite. Learners state *how* quantities relate, test the relation with data/graphs/tables, and use a first formula as a relationship — not as a plug-in drill. This matches the pack's central STEM dial: T1 = qualitative/observable, T2 = relational/proportional, T3 = formal/quantified (Lane 1 §6, [Inferred] synthesis anchored in §1d/§2e).

### Math — T2
- **Belongs:** Ratios and unit rates, proportional relationships, percent as rate, rational numbers including negatives, dividing fractions in context, variable expressions (Gr 6), multi-step equations (Gr 7), linear equations, introduction to systems, geometry including scale drawings, area/volume in context, Pythagorean theorem as a distance tool, statistics and probability, and functions formalised in Grade 8 as "each input has exactly one output" (Lane 1 §1a, domains Gr 6–8 [Low 1/1]; Gr 6/7/8 sequencing [Low 2/2]; §1d functions anchor [Low 1/1]).
- **Depth:** Quantitative-lite. Solve with tables, graphs, and equations; explain *why* a proportion holds. No formal proof, no quadratic factoring systems, no trigonometric functions as identities.
- **Algebra/functions on-ramp — RULING [Inferred]:** Three-step ramp inside T2: Gr 6 = variables stand for numbers; Gr 7 = proportional relationships become $y = kx$ in tables/graphs; Gr 8 = function definition, linear models, and early systems. Ceiling is Algebra I *readiness*, with an accelerated lane to Algebra I in Gr 8 where districts already run one — TX Advanced track §111.31 Gr 8 / Algebra I (Lane 2 §1a, [Moderate 1/1]); LAUSD Highly Accelerated 8th = Algebra 1 (Lane 3 §2a, [Moderate 1/1]). Core T2 does **not** require Algebra I completion for all learners.

### Physics — T2
- **Belongs:** Forces and motion as dependence, energy transfer and conservation in everyday systems, waves/sound/light basics, magnets/electric interactions qualitatively.
- **Depth — hard ceiling:** MS-PS2-2: "change in an object's motion depends on the sum of the forces on the object and the mass of the object" — evidence for dependence, *without* the formal equation as the assessed claim (Lane 1 §2e, [Low 1/1], corroborated). Kinetic energy at T2 **is** quantitative-relational: KE proportional to mass and to the square of speed, MS-PS3-1 endpoint by end of Gr 8 (Lane 1 §2c/§2e, [Moderate 1/1]). Temperature = average kinetic energy of particles (Lane 1 §2c, [Moderate 1/1]).
- **Not T2:** Quantified $F_{net}=ma$ problem sets, gravitation/Coulomb mathematical representations — those are HS-PS2-1 / HS-PS2-4, T3 (Lane 1 §2e, [Moderate 1/1]).

### Chemistry — T2
- **Belongs:** Atoms → molecules model (substances made from different types of atoms combining in various ways, molecules from two to thousands of atoms, MS-PS1-1), characteristic properties identify pure substances (MS-PS1-2/3), chemical reactions as rearrangement with mass conserved qualitatively, mixtures/dissolving, particle explanation of states (Lane 1 §2c PS1.A progression 6–8, [Moderate 1/1]).
- **Depth:** Model-and-evidence, not symbolic drill. Learners draw particle diagrams before/after and argue from observable property change.
- **Not T2:** Atomic substructure (protons/neutrons/electrons, periodic table by proton number = HS-PS1-1, T3), mole / Avogadro conversion (HS-PS1-7 only, T3 ceiling anchor), nuclear processes PS1.C (Lane 1 §2e/§2f, [Moderate 1/1]).

### General Knowledge — Geography / Earth & Space / Interdisciplinary — T2
- **Belongs:** MS-ESS1/2/3 — Earth systems, plate tectonics as system interaction, water's role in surface processes, weather/climate patterns, Earth–Sun–Moon system, geological time scale from rock strata organising Earth's history by end of Gr 8 (Lane 1 §2c ESS1.C, [Low 1/1]; §2d mapping, [Inferred]).
- **Depth:** Systems-relational. Learners trace cause chains across spheres (e.g., ocean current → climate → settlement), use maps at multiple scales. Formal isotopic dating, orbital mechanics calculations = T3.
- **Note:** Lane 4 shows GK is the thinnest supply at 10–18: only 3 items (Lane 4 §a, [Tested]). T2 GK must be built, not just re-tagged — see §5.

### Social Studies — History / Civics / Economics / Human Geography — T2
- **Belongs:** C3 Band 6–8 for all four disciplines (Lane 1 §3b, [Inferred] mapping). History sequence in lived practice: World (Gr 6) → World Medieval/Early Modern + early U.S. (Gr 7) → U.S. History to Reconstruction / Growth and Conflict (Gr 8) — TX: Gr 6 World Cultures, Gr 7 Texas History, Gr 8 U.S. to 1877 (Lane 2 §1a, [Moderate 2/2]); NY: Gr 6 Eastern Hemisphere, Gr 7–8 U.S. and NY History I/II (Lane 2 §2a, [Moderate 1/1]); CA: Gr 6 Ancient Civilizations, Gr 7 Medieval/Early Modern, Gr 8 U.S. Growth and Conflict (Lane 2 §3a, [Moderate 1/1]); districts converge on Gr 6 World History — CPS Gr 6 World History to Age of Exploration (Lane 3 §3c, [Moderate 1/1]), Miami Gr 6 World History (Lane 3 §4c, [Low 1/1]).
- **Depth — verb is the dial:** C3 6–8 = *assess / analyse purposes and consequences* (e.g., D2.Civ.12.6-8 assess rules/laws as means of addressing public problems; D2.Civ.13.6-8 analyse purposes, implementation, consequences of public policies), vs 3–5 describe/explain and 9–12 evaluate/critique complex causation (Lane 1 §3a/§3c, [Low 1/1]).
- **Civics placement — RULING [Inferred]:** Civics sits **at T2, anchored in Grade 7**, not held entirely to a T3 semester. Evidence: Miami-Dade runs a full Gr 7 Civics year built around the Grade 7 Civics End-of-Course Exam (Lane 3 §4c, [Moderate 1/1]); CPS places Civics at Grades 8 *and* 11 (Lane 3 §3c, [Moderate 2/2]); NYC runs Civics for All as a companion strand alongside Passport (Lane 3 §1c, [Moderate 1/1]). Counter-evidence: TX/NY/CA all require a government/civics semester at T3 (Lane 2 D8, [High 3/3]). **Resolution:** Meridian T2 teaches foundational civics as its own case strand (institutions, Constitution, participation, law's impact) at C3 6–8 depth; T3 then teaches the formal government course. Following Miami on *timing*, C3 on *depth*, and the three states on *not* replacing the T3 course. Do not design a high-stakes civics test into Meridian — a standalone civics-test graduation requirement was not verified in any state in this pass (Lane 2 D8: Data not available).

### Journals — Applied Field Notes — T2
- **Belongs:** No national discipline exists for Journals (Lane 1 §1b/§6, [Inferred]; Lane 2 §6: no TX/NY/CA standard names Journals). T2 Journals = process tier: CCSS Reading Informational Text Gr 6–8 band — cite several pieces of textual evidence (RI.7.1), determine central ideas and summarise objectively (RI.7.2), trace/evaluate argument and claims (RI.7.8) (Lane 1 §1b, [Low 1/1]) — plus NGSS SEPs/CCCs and ETS1 engineering design (MS-ETS1) as the doing layer (Lane 1 §2d, [Inferred] mapping).
- **Depth:** Learner as investigator: pose question (C3 Dimension 1), gather/sort evidence (Dimension 3), communicate conclusion / take informed action (Dimension 4) (Lane 1 §3b, [Inferred]). Text complexity = 6–8 band, not 9–10.

### Science architecture — spiral-integrated vs year-blocked — RULING
**CONFIRM spiral-integrated for T2. Do not block one discipline per year. [Inferred, strongly evidence-backed]**

- NYC SCALE Science 6–8 "follows an integrated model in which earth and space science, life science, and physical science are intertwined within each unit of study" — distributed across all three middle grades, not blocked (Lane 3 §1b, [Moderate 1/1]).
- LAUSD documents three programme options per grade (HMH / Amplify / Stanford SCALE) for each of Grades 6, 7, 8, all NGSS middle-school programmes, maps titled by grade not discipline (Lane 3 §2b, [Moderate 1/1] for existence; integration [Inferred]).
- CPS Skyline Science 6–8: life, physical and earth science all present across 6–8, integrated not blocked by year (Lane 3 §3b, [Moderate 2/2]; discipline mix [Inferred] from guide).
- Miami-Dade: pacing guides organised by four Bodies of Knowledge — Nature of Science; Earth & Space; Life; Physical — across K–5 and 6–8 every year (Lane 3 §4b, [Low 1/1]).
- State layer agrees in form if not wording: CA's preferred model for 6–8 is the Integrated Learning Progression (Lane 2 §3a, [High 2/2]); NY is banded 6–8, not grade-assigned (Lane 2 §2a, [Moderate 1/1]); TX is the outlier with grade-by-grade integrated science §§112.26–112.28 — still integrated *within* each grade, not discipline-blocked (Lane 2 §1a, [Moderate 1/1]).

**Product translation [Inferred]:** Meridian keeps Physics, Chemistry, and GK as *picker categories* (learner choice), but the T2 *sequence* inside each year spirals: every T2 year contains Physics-lens, Chemistry-lens, Earth-lens, and Life-lens cases revisited at rising relational depth. A "Gr 6 = Earth only, Gr 7 = Life only, Gr 8 = Physical only" year-block would contradict every documented district in the pack. No dissent.

## 2. THE 10–18 SPLIT — RE-TAGGING RULE FOR THE 60 ITEMS

Lane 4 fact: the current `10-18` band holds 60 items — Math 28, Physics 9, Chemistry 8, GK 3, Social Studies 5, Journals 7 — with **no per-item grade/age granularity inside the band**; `AgeGroup` is the only axis, so T2 vs T3 cannot be separated without re-tagging all 60 (Lane 4 §a/§d, [Tested]/[Inferred]).

**General test [Inferred]:** Tag T2 if a typical Gr 8 learner can solve it by stating and testing a relationship (table/graph/model/evidence) *without* a T3 formalism listed in the right column; tag T3 if the solution *requires* that formalism. One testable sentence per category:

| Category | T2 vs T3 distinguishing sentence [Inferred] |
|---|---|
| **Math** | An item is T2 if it is solved with proportional reasoning, rational-number operations, or a linear model/function, and T3 if it requires non-linear functions, formal trigonometry ratios, multi-step symbolic manipulation, or proof. |
| **Physics** | An item is T2 if it asks for evidence that motion/energy *depends on* force, mass, or speed (including KE ∝ mass·speed² as a stated relation), and T3 if it requires calculating with $F_{net}=ma$, gravitation, or Coulomb's law. |
| **Chemistry** | An item is T2 if it is solved with an atoms→molecules particle model and characteristic properties, and T3 if it requires subatomic structure, periodic-table reasoning by proton number, or mole conversion. |
| **General Knowledge** | An item is T2 if it is solved by tracing a relationship inside an Earth/human system on a map or model, and T3 if it requires quantitative Earth-science modelling, isotopic/geologic dating methods, or evaluating globalisation/resource conflict at C3 9–12 depth. |
| **Social Studies** | An item is T2 if the verb is *assess/analyse purpose and consequence* of a rule, event, or pattern (C3 6–8), and T3 if the verb is *evaluate/critique* multiple complex causes or a secondary argument (C3 9–12). |
| **Journals** | An item is T2 if the learner cites evidence from a 6–8-band text/data set to support a claim, and T3 if the learner must synthesise conflicting sources, critique a secondary work, or produce an AP Seminar/Research-style argument. |

**Worked application to Lane 4 inventory [Inferred]:** Likely T2 keeps: Math unit-rate / proportion / linear-model / percent / scale-drawing / coordinates / Pythagoras-as-distance; Physics push:mass, ramp:higher-stored, energy:transfer, magnet:closer; Chemistry states:particles, mixtures:dissolve, reactions:weight, states:road-salt. Likely T3 moves: Math trig:ratios / trig:missing-side / trig:missing-angle at formal ratio depth, rates:rearrange at symbolic depth; Journals `:stretch` variants (see §3). Full item-by-item re-tag: Data not available from pack alone — requires reading each item's `objective`/`why` against the sentences above.

## 3. BLOAT CUTS — WHAT A CONCISE T2 DOES NOT CARRY [Inferred]

1. **Formal trigonometry drill set.** Bearings and angle intuition can stay as navigation context; sine/cosine/tangent ratio problem sets belong to T3 Math. They inflate the current 10–18 Math list (4+ trig ids, Lane 4 §b, [Tested]) beyond the Gr 6–8 CCSS domains, where Functions only arrives in Gr 8 (Lane 1 §1a, [Low 1/1]).
2. **Procedural algorithm repetition.** Standard-algorithm division, multi-digit drill, and decimal-shift drill as standalone cases — fluency practice, not "Why Do We Learn This?" cases. Keep one contextual case each; cut duplicates.
3. **Journals `:stretch` duplicates.** 4 of 16 Journals records are `:stretch` variants of other records; effective distinct-topic count is lower than 16 (Lane 4 §d, [Tested]/[Inferred]). At T2 keep the stronger version, cut the stretch copy — a concise curriculum does not pad counts with re-skins.
4. **Trivia GK.** Opera-years-ago / landmark-age comparison items (Lane 4 §b GK 7–10 pattern extending into thin GK supply) teach no T2 system relationship. Cut in favour of Earth-systems cases (§5). GK has only 3 items at 10–18 — every slot must carry a system.
5. **T3 formalisms pulled down.** Mole conversions, $F=ma$ calculation sets, subatomic structure, formal proof, AP-style stoichiometry — all explicitly bounded to T3 by Lane 1 §2e. Including them at T2 is bloat *and* miscalibration.
6. **State-specific history content hard-coded as national T2.** TX Gr 7 Texas History second full year has no NY/CA equivalent (Lane 2 D4, [Moderate 2/3]). Keep a state-agnostic "My State / My Community" slot; do not build 50 state curricula into core T2.

## 5. CASE-FILE PROMPTS — T2 COMPLETE [All Inferred]

Format per prompt: **Hook question** — real-world situation — *concept taught*. Reuse flagged against Lane 4 §b inventory; otherwise Extension.

### Math (4)
1. **"Why does the cheaper-looking phone plan end up costing more?"** — Comparing two phone/data plans with a base fee plus per-GB charge over a school semester. — *Proportional relationships and linear models: unit rate, $y = mx + b$ as a relationship.* [Extension — extends inventoried `math-applied:rates:linear-model` to a two-plan comparison]
2. **"Why do goalkeepers and game designers both care about a fair coin that isn't fair?"** — Testing whether a loot-box / free-throw / board-game spinner outcome matches its claimed chances over many trials. — *Probability and statistical inference: relative frequency approaches theoretical probability.* [Extension — no probability item in Lane 4 Math 10–18 list]
3. **"Why can your account go below zero — and what does 'below' even mean?"** — A lunch-card / savings balance, temperature drop, and elevation below sea level in one week of a 12-year-old's life. — *The Number System: negative rational numbers on a number line, operations with integers.* [Extension — no negative-number item in Lane 4 Math 10–18 list]
4. **"Why does the shortcut across the park actually save distance?"** — Measuring the diagonal path across a rectangular field vs walking two sides, checked with string and a map. — *Pythagorean theorem as a distance tool (Gr 8).* [Reuses inventoried topic: `math-applied:geometry:pythagoras` / `distance-points`]

### Physics (4)
1. **"Why does doubling your bike speed quadruple how hard it is to stop?"** — Braking distance and crash energy on a bike/scooter at 10 vs 20 km/h in the school zone. — *Kinetic energy ∝ mass × speed² (MS-PS3-1) as a relationship, not a calculation drill.* [Extension — deepens inventoried `physics:ramp:faster-hits-harder` (7–10) to T2 relational depth]
2. **"Why is the same push enough for an empty cart but not a full one?"** — Pushing loaded vs unloaded carts / sleds and measuring how motion changes. — *MS-PS2-2: motion change depends on sum of forces and mass — evidence for dependence.* [Reuses inventoried topic: `physics:push:mass`]
3. **"Why does a blanket 'make' you warm if it makes no heat?"** — Comparing temperature loss of warm water in insulated vs uninsulated bottles over a class period. — *Energy transfer, temperature as average kinetic energy of particles, systems and insulation.* [Extension — extends inventoried `physics:energy:transfer`]
4. **"Why can you hear the game through the wall but not see it?"** — Sound travelling through air, wood, and water vs light blocked by the same wall at a sleepover / swim meet. — *Waves: sound needs a medium, wave properties differ by type (MS-PS4).* [Reuses inventoried topic: `physics:sound:medium`]

### Chemistry (4)
1. **"Why does salt melt ice on the road but also rust the car?"** — Winter road treatment in a cold-climate town, followed through spring. — *Mixtures, dissolving, and properties change in context; trade-offs of a chemical solution.* [Reuses inventoried topic: `chemistry:states:road-salt` + `chemistry:reactions:rust`]
2. **"Why does the sealed bag weigh the same after the fizzy reaction pops?"** — Baking soda + vinegar reacting inside a sealed bag on a kitchen scale. — *Conservation of mass in a chemical reaction, argued from particle rearrangement (MS-PS1).* [Reuses inventoried topic: `chemistry:reactions:weight`]
3. **"Why can you un-mix trail mix but not un-bake a cake?"** — Separating a snack mix by hand vs trying to reverse baking, melting, and rusting. — *Physical vs chemical change; characteristic properties identify substances.* [Extension — extends inventoried `chemistry:reactions:new-look` / `chemistry:materials:identify`]
4. **"Why does perfume reach the back of the room if nobody blew it there?"** — A scent, food colouring in water, and steam from a hot drink spreading without a fan. — *Particle model of matter: atoms/molecules in motion explain diffusion and states.* [Reuses inventoried topic: `chemistry:states:particles`]

### General Knowledge — Geography / Earth & Space (4)
1. **"Why do earthquakes shake one side of your state and not the other?"** — Mapping recent quakes and volcanoes against plate boundaries to judge risk for a school trip. — *Plate tectonics and large-scale system interactions (MS-ESS2).* [Extension — no tectonics item in Lane 4 GK inventory]
2. **"Why does it rain on your hike but stay dry 20 km away?"** — A weekend storm, a rain shadow behind a mountain range, and a local forecast that got one town right and one wrong. — *Weather systems, water in Earth's surface processes, air-mass interaction (MS-ESS2).* [Extension — extends inventoried `chemistry:water:cycle` into Earth systems]
3. **"Why do we get seasons — and why is it summer in Sydney at Christmas?"** — Comparing daylight, Sun angle, and temperature in the learner's town vs Paris/Sydney across the year. — *Earth–Sun system, axial tilt, orbit patterns (MS-ESS1).* [Extension — extends inventoried `general-knowledge:mixed:paris-sydney-flight` from flight trivia to system explanation]
4. **"Why does the cliff show stripes — and what story do the stripes tell?"** — Reading layered rock and fossils on a canyon / road-cut visit to order events in Earth's past. — *Geological time scale from rock strata (end-of-Gr 8 endpoint).* [Extension — no strata item in Lane 4 GK inventory]

### Social Studies (4)
1. **"Why does your town have laws nobody voted for directly — and how could kids change one?"** — A local rule (skate park hours, library fines) traced from proposal to enforcement, with a petition that worked. — *Civics C3 6–8: assess rules/laws as means of addressing public problems (D2.Civ.12.6-8); institutions, participation.* [Extension — no civics item in Lane 4 Social Studies inventory, which is geography-math only]
2. **"Why did the price of the sold-out sneakers double overnight?"** — A limited drop, resale listings, and a store that chose not to raise prices. — *Economics: supply, demand, incentives, and consequences for different groups.* [Extension — no economics item in Lane 4 Social Studies inventory]
3. **"Why did cities grow where two rivers meet — and why do some shrink now?"** — Comparing a river-junction city, a port, and the learner's own town on maps across 200 years. — *Human geography: population patterns, human–environment interaction, density in context.* [Reuses inventoried topic: `social-studies:region:density` + `route:scale-ratio`, extended to historical causation]
4. **"Why did traders risk a desert crossing for salt and gold?"** — Following a medieval trade route (Andean roads / trans-Saharan / Silk Road choice) and what each side gained. — *History Gr 6–7: exchange networks, change and continuity, multiple perspectives on the same event.* [Extension — extends inventoried Journals `people-places:andean-roads` into Social Studies history]

### Journals — Applied Field Notes (4)
1. **"Why should you trust this viral 'science' post — or not?"** — A viral health/space claim shared in a group chat, checked against two sources and the original data. — *RI.7.8 trace/evaluate argument and claims; C3 Dimension 3 evaluating sources and using evidence.* [Extension — no source-evaluation item in Lane 4 Journals inventory]
2. **"Why did our bridge hold 2 kg yesterday and fail today?"** — A classroom spaghetti/card bridge design challenge, tested, redesigned, and retested with a field log. — *ETS1 engineering design (MS-ETS1): define problem, develop solutions, optimise; SEP data practices.* [Extension — no ETS1 item in Lane 4 Journals inventory]
3. **"Why is the creek behind school cloudy after rain — and who needs to know?"** — Learners sample/observe local water, log weather, photograph change, and write a note a city worker could act on. — *Field investigation: question → evidence → communicated conclusion (C3 Dimensions 1/3/4); Earth-systems observation.* [Extension — new field-note topic beyond Lane 4 Journals list]
4. **"Why did Eratosthenes measure the whole Earth with a stick and a shadow?"** — Replicating the shadow measurement with a partner class / simulated second location and comparing results. — *Applied measurement, evidence from indirect observation, historical science as field notes.* [Reuses inventoried topic: `people-places:eratosthenes-earth`]

## 6. AGREEMENTS & DISSENTS

**Agreements [Inferred]:**
- With Lane 1's quantification dial (T2 = relational) and its hard T3 anchors (mole, $F=ma$, substructure). No T2 case should require them.
- With Lane 2 D6: T2 science tagged by topic + readiness band, not locked single grade.
- With Lane 3's unanimous district picture: integrated middle-school science, Gr 6 World History opening, Algebra 1 anchored at Gr 9 with Gr 8 acceleration.
- With Lane 4's mapping-risk warning: the 60-item 10–18 band cannot be split by data alone; the §2 sentences are the human re-tagging instrument.

**Dissents / positions [Inferred]:**
- **Dissent from year-blocked science in any form** — including a "gentle" version where Meridian sequences all Physics cases before Chemistry cases inside T2. The pack shows intertwining *within units*, not just within years (Lane 3 NYC SCALE, [Moderate 1/1]). Spiral means interleaved delivery, not ordered silos.
- **Dissent from holding civics to T3 only.** The three-state T3 semester (Lane 2 D8, [High 3/3]) describes a *course requirement*, not evidence that 11–14-year-olds should meet civics for the first time at 17. Miami's Gr 7 year + EOC (Lane 3, [Moderate 1/1]) is the stronger T2 precedent.
- **Life Science position — explicit:** Life Science (NGSS LS1–LS4) is a full discipline in every band with no Meridian home (Lane 1 §2f gap flag); the ACT Science benchmark course is Biology (Lane 1 §5a, [Moderate 1/1]); AP Biology exists with no Meridian home (Lane 1 §4b); and every documented district teaches life science *inside* T2 integrated science (Lane 3 NYC/CPS/Miami/LAUSD). **Position: Meridian must not claim NGSS completeness at T2 while LS is homeless, and must not spiral "all science" while omitting the strand districts spiral most visibly.** Interim ruling [Inferred]: house LS at T2 as a *named sub-strand inside General Knowledge — Interdisciplinary* (Lane 1 §2f option b), with its own case count and prompts in the next design round, and put the permanent choice — new 7th category vs permanent GK sub-strand — to the owner as a structural decision. Declaring LS out of scope (option c) is rejected for T2: a middle-school product that skips cells, ecosystems, heredity, and evolution does not match any classroom in the pack.
- **Caution, not dissent, on Journals counts:** Lane 4 counts 7 Journals items at 10–18, but 4 Journals records overall are `:stretch` variants (Lane 4 §d). Council should vote T2 Journals supply on distinct topics, not raw counts. Exact T2 stretch count: Data not available from pack summary alone.

*End of memo.*
