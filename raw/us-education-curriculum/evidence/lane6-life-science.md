# Lane 6 — Life Science evidence workstream (2026-11-25)

Purpose. This lane gives the Living World strand its verified standards
anchors. It follows the evidence-record format in §6 of the owner's
implementation guide. Every record below is anchored on the official
NGSS site (nextgenscience.org), the primary source for NGSS
performance expectations (PEs). PE wording is paraphrased to the
anchor concept, not quoted at length. The full PE text lives at the
cited source.

Confidence rule applied (revised standard, see the revision document).
A single authoritative primary source that directly states a PE is
[High 1/1] for that narrow fact. Claims that combine several PEs into
a tier statement carry the tag of their weakest record.

Source used for all records in this pass.
- Title: NGSS "Read the Standards" search and DCI arrangement pages.
- URL: https://www.nextgenscience.org/search-standards?keys=&tid_3%5B0%5D=96&page=2
- URL: https://www.nextgenscience.org/dci-arrangement/hs-ls2-ecosystems-interactions-energy-and-dynamics
- Type: national-framework. Origin: primary. Year: NGSS released 2013,
  pages current at retrieval on 2026-11-25.
