## 1. T4 DEFINITION RULING

### Ruling [Inferred]

**T4 is a deliberately small, application-first life-domains layer — anchored below by the ACT readiness floor, oriented above by AAC&U LEAP/VALUE outcomes, entered through a real adult decision — not a remediation track, not an AP extension, and not a parallel college syllabus.**

In one line: *T4 = formal tools from T1–T3, re-entered at the moment an adult must spend, build, dose, vote, travel, or judge a claim — proved by the decision, not by the next topic in sequence.*

### Why each single-option definition fails on the pack's own evidence

**Option A — ACT-floor remediation alone: REJECTED as the definition (retained as the floor).**
- ACT benchmarks are explicitly a floor, not a curriculum: "ACT benchmarks define the floor of T4 / ceiling of T3, not a T4 curriculum" — Lane 1 §5a [Inferred in Lane 1, from ACT official table, Moderate 1/1].
- The floor values themselves are evidenced: Mathematics 22 → College Algebra; Reading 22 → American History / other History / Psychology / Sociology / Political Science / Economics; Science 23 → Biology; STEM 26 → Calculus, Chemistry, Biology, Physics, Engineering — Lane 1 §5a, ACT official [Moderate 1/1].
- A remediation-only T4 would re-teach T3 content under a "college" label. Lane 1 §5a warns against exactly this: T4 "should not re-teach T3 content labelled as 'college.'" [Inferred in Lane 1]
- Adult-learning consequence [Inferred]: an adult who arrives for a mortgage, a dosage, or a ballot question and is routed through a College Algebra readiness sequence will leave. Remediation belongs in T4 only as a *just-in-time diagnostic detour* inside a case ("you need this one tool — here it is, now decide"), never as the tier's organising principle.

**Option B — AP-bridge extension alone: REJECTED as the definition (retained as an on-ramp).**
- AP is evidenced as "college-level coursework offered in high school" and as "the T3 ceiling that earns T4 credit — the practical bridge" — Lane 1 §4a/§5d [Moderate 1/1 for course names from the College Board listing; bridge role is Inferred in Lane 1].
- AP course names mapping to Meridian are verified (AP Physics 1/2 and C, AP Chemistry, AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Precalculus, AP Statistics, AP Human Geography, AP US/World/European History, AP US/Comparative Government, AP Macro/Microeconomics, AP Seminar/Research) — Lane 1 §4b [Moderate 1/1].
- But AP's detailed content cannot ground a T4 curriculum on this pack: current Course and Exam Description unit weights and Big Idea wording are **Data not available** — Lane 1 §4d and §8 register. Building T4 as "AP, continued" would rest the tier on documents the pack did not retrieve.
- Adult-learning consequence [Inferred]: AP is an exam-and-credit instrument for enrolled high-school students. Most adult learners need no credit and will not sit an exam; an AP-shaped T4 optimises for a credential its audience is not seeking.

**Option C — Application-first life-domains alone: ACCEPTED as the organising principle, but only inside the composite.**
- No lane in the pack documents an existing national adult life-domains curriculum — see the negative evidence below. Option C is therefore a design construction [Inferred], not a discovered standard, and this memo labels it as such.
- It is the only option consistent with the voluntary framework the pack *does* evidence for T4: AAC&U LEAP outcomes include "Integrative and Applied Learning," "civic knowledge and engagement — local and global," and quantitative literacy defined as analysing quantitative information, completing calculations "to answer meaningful questions," making judgements based on quantitative data, and communicating results — Lane 1 §5b [Low 1/1 — university/state republications of AAC&U LEAP and the VALUE QL rubric; one origin family]. Note the confidence: **Low**. The composite leans on LEAP/VALUE for *orientation* (what kind of capability T4 builds), never as a mandate, and never described as "the national college standard" — Lane 1 §5b status flag explicitly forbids that presentation [Inferred assessment in Lane 1].

### The negative evidence — what T4 cannot claim to be

- "There is no U.S. national general-education curriculum for college/adult learners in Math, Physics, Chemistry, Geography, or Social Studies comparable to CCSS/NGSS/C3 for K–12 retrieved in this lane" — Lane 1 §5c. Specific T4 topic progressions beyond AP/first-year college: **Data not available** — Lane 1 §5c, §8.
- State frameworks end at Grade 12. Every T4 cell in Lane 2's TX, NY, and CA mapping tables reads "Data not available," and Lane 2 §6.1 states: "K-12 state standards do not define a T4 band" — Lane 2 §§1b, 2b, 3b, 4, 6.1.
- Districts document nothing for T4: "all five are K-12 systems" and "no data in any district document (all K-12)" — Lane 3 header and coverage summary.
- Any claim that a particular adult topic sequence (e.g., a national adult Physics or Chemistry progression) is standards-required: **Data not available.**
- Any claim about adult learner completion, persistence, or andragogy effect sizes: **Data not available** in this pack. The motivational premise in the brief (adults arrive with a job, a mortgage, a vote, a body) is treated in this memo as a design premise supplied by the council charge, not as a pack-evidenced finding, and all reasoning from it is labelled [Inferred].

### The defensible composite [Inferred]

| Layer | Source in pack | Function in T4 |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor (diagnostic, not a gate)** | ACT benchmarks — Lane 1 §5a [Moderate 1/1] | Defines what a case may *assume* on entry (College Algebra readiness, Math 22; social-science reading, Reading 22; first-year science, Science 23 / STEM 26). If a case needs a tool below the floor, the case teaches that one tool inline — it does not reroute the learner to T3. |
| **Orientation (voluntary, labelled as such)** | AAC&U LEAP + VALUE QL — Lane 1 §5b [Low 1/1] | Defines what a case must *produce*: a judgement from quantitative/evidence-based information, communicated — plus civic knowledge and integrative applied learning. Used as reference framework only. |
| **On-ramp (optional, for credit-seekers)** | AP as T3 ceiling / T4 bridge — Lane 1 §4, §5d [Moderate 1/1 for names] | Learners who *do* want college credit enter via the AP-bridge topics. This is a side door, not the front door. |
| **Organising principle (the tier itself)** | No national source — design construction [Inferred] | Life-domains: money, home, health, work, citizenship, travel. Entry is a decision the learner already faces; the concept is taught because the decision needs it. Sequence is by decision urgency and tool-dependence, not by disciplinary order. |

## 2. T4 CALIBRATION PER CATEGORY

**General calibration rule [Inferred]:** T4 depth = *the formal tool, used once, correctly, on the learner's own numbers* — then the judgement stated in plain language. T4 never goes deeper than the decision requires, and never shallower than the tool that makes the decision safe. The T3 ceilings from Lane 1 are hard upper bounds for the *core* (anything above them is AP-extension and must be labelled as such): quantified F = ma is T3 (HS-PS2-1) — Lane 1 §2e [Moderate 1/1]; the mole as atomic→macroscopic conversion is a T3 ceiling anchor (HS-PS1-7), with NGSS explicitly bounding assessment away from complex reactions and rote stoichiometry — Lane 1 §2e [Moderate 1/1].

### Math — quantitative literacy for money and measurement decisions
- **Stance [Inferred]:** VALUE QL in adult dress: read the chart/contract, estimate, calculate, judge, communicate — Lane 1 §5b [Low 1/1]. Entry assumption: College Algebra readiness (ACT Math 22) — Lane 1 §5a [Moderate 1/1]; cases that need less must still *end* at a judgement, not at an arithmetic answer.
- **Depth [Inferred]:** Percent, ratio, and unit-rate fluency applied to interest (APR vs APY, amortisation intuition — total cost, not just monthly payment), tax and tip as percent-of-what discipline, inflation-adjusted comparisons, expected value and base rates for insurance/lottery/medical-test claims, and reading graphs with truncated axes. Formal algebra appears only as rearranging a formula the decision owns (e.g., solving a loan or dosage formula for the unknown the learner controls) — continuous with the T2/T3 rates-and-rearrange line already in inventory (`math-applied:rates:rearrange`, `math-applied:rates:linear-model`, 10–18 band) — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested].
- **Refuse at this tier:** symbolic manipulation for its own sake; calculus in the core (Calculus sits behind the STEM 26 threshold — Lane 1 §5a [Moderate 1/1] — and belongs to the AP-bridge side door).

### Physics — energy, motion, and the built home
- **Stance [Inferred]:** The physics of bills, vehicles, and houses. Energy is the lead concept because it is the one adults pay for monthly; NGSS PS3.D is literally "Energy in Chemical Processes and Everyday Life" — Lane 1 §2b DCI list [Moderate 1/1].
- **Depth [Inferred]:** Quantitative but single-step: kWh as a unit you buy, power × time, insulation/heat-loss reasoning, braking distance scaling (KE proportional to mass and to the square of speed is the end-of-Grade-8 endpoint, MS-PS3 — Lane 1 §2e [Moderate 1/1] — so T4 may *use* v² scaling in a driving decision without re-deriving it), leverage and mechanical advantage for moving/lifting, ramp slope as ratio for accessibility. No multi-body problem sets; no calculus-based mechanics (AP Physics C is the bridge track, not the core — Lane 1 §4b/§4c [Moderate 1/1 / Inferred]).
- **Continuity:** builds directly on the three inventoried 18+ items (`physics:energy:missing`, `physics:arrive:adult-time`, `physics:ramp:adult-ratio`) — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]. See §5 reuse flags.

### Chemistry — the chemistry of products, food, and the body
- **Stance [Inferred]:** Concentration, dilution, and reaction-as-change, in service of label-reading and safe use: cleaning products, water treatment, cooking, medication-adjacent literacy (what a dose concentration *means* — not medical advice; see anti-bloat, §4).
- **Depth [Inferred]:** Deliberately below the T3 ceiling. T4 core = concentration as amount-per-volume, dilution ratios, pH as a log-scale idea (a label claim you can sanity-check), reaction rate as "why temperature/concentration change how fast" — continuous with inventoried 18+ items `chemistry:reactions:rate`, `chemistry:reactions:mass`, `chemistry:reactions:explain` — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]. The mole appears only if a case genuinely needs it (e.g., reading a lab result in mmol/L), introduced as a counting unit, never as a stoichiometry unit. Rote stoichiometry, limiting reactant, and percent yield are beyond NGSS HS-PS1-7's stated boundary and are AP extension — Lane 1 §2e/§4c [Moderate 1/1 / Inferred] — excluded from the T4 core.

### General Knowledge (Geography / Earth & Space / Interdisciplinary) — place, planet, and systems judgement
- **Stance [Inferred]:** Spatial and Earth-systems literacy for travel, climate-exposed property, and news comprehension. This category carries the pack's geography split honestly: C3 D2.Geo is human/spatial, NGSS ESS is physical Earth system, AP Human Geography is human — Lane 1 §6.5 [Inferred in Lane 1]. T4 GK cases must declare which geography they are teaching (physical system vs human pattern) rather than blending them into trivia.
- **Depth [Inferred]:** Great-circle distance and time-zone arithmetic for real itineraries (both already inventoried at 18+: `general-knowledge:mixed:great-circle`, `social-studies:compare:time-zones` at 10–18 — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]), map-projection distortion as a judgement skill (inventoried: `social-studies:region:flat-map-area`, 18+ — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]), river/flow and water systems for flood and drought decisions (inventoried: `general-knowledge:mixed:falls-flow`, 18+ — Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]), and Earth-system cause chains (hazard → exposure → cost) at the ESS3 "Earth and Human Activity" level — Lane 1 §2b [Moderate 1/1].
- **Health/biology housing:** see §6 — this memo places a bounded applied-health strand here (with Journals), [Inferred].

### Social Studies (History / Civics / Economics / Human Geography) — the citizen's operating system
- **Stance [Inferred]:** C3's own endpoint logic, one step past T3. C3's verb ladder runs describe/explain (3–5) → assess/analyse consequences (6–8) → evaluate/critique complex causation and secondary arguments (9–12) — Lane 1 §3c [Inferred in Lane 1, from indicators at Low 1/1]. T4's verb is **decide and act**: evaluate a ballot measure's costs/benefits across groups (the 9–12 economics indicator D2.Eco.1.9-12 already frames choices this way — Lane 1 §3a [Low 1/1]), read a pay stub / tax form / lease as primary sources, and judge a historical analogy offered in public argument.
- **Depth [Inferred]:** Personal finance is *inside* this category's adult remit, and the state evidence shows its placement is unstable even at T3 — fused with economics in TX (0.5 credit, 2/3 PFL + 1/3 economics), standalone in CA for 2030–31 graduates, inside the economics course title in NY — Lane 2 §5 D3 [High 2/2 TX/NY; Moderate 1/1 CA]. T4 consequence [Inferred]: build Economics and Personal Finance as *separable* cases (Lane 2's own design consequence [Inferred]) so the adult tier never hard-codes one state's fusion. Compound-interest and credit-cost cases are shared with Math by design (Math owns the tool, Social Studies owns the institution) — cross-listed, not duplicated (see §4).
- **Convergent spine available:** all three states require U.S. History + a government/civics semester + an economics semester at HS — Lane 2 §5 D5 [High 3/3]. T4 may therefore assume that spine as prior exposure and build *application* on top of it, without re-teaching it (§4).

### Journals (applied field notes) — the process tier, now with the learner's own life as the field
- **Stance [Inferred]:** Journals has no national discipline of its own; its anchors are process anchors (CCSS informational reading + note-taking, NGSS practices/ETS1, C3 Dimensions 1/3/4: question → evidence → communicated conclusion/action, AP Seminar/Research at the ceiling) and it should be designed "as a process tier that rises with the other categories" — Lane 1 §6.4 [Inferred in Lane 1]. Lane 2 concurs: Journals "is a Meridian-original category and should be designed as a cross-cutting practice layer, not a standards-aligned subject" — Lane 2 §4 [Inferred].
- **T4 depth [Inferred]:** The adult journal is a *decision record*: a home-energy log, a travel field note that tests a guidebook claim against observation, a workplace measurement note, a health log read against a reference range. The skill taught is the C3/Lane 1 process at adult stakes — frame the question, gather evidence you can defend, record it so a second person (a contractor, a clinician, a future self) can act on it. AP Seminar/Research is the ceiling reference, not the model — Lane 1 §4b [Moderate 1/1 for the course names; Inferred for the mapping].
- **Structural warning from inventory [Tested]:** Journals is the only subject with no `scenes.ts` (caption-text leads instead of clue scenes), and 4 of its 16 records are `:stretch` variants — Lane 4 §(c), §(d) [Tested/Inferred]. T4 Journals design must budget for scene/clip production, not just item writing, or the new cases will ship in the same degraded format.

## 3. THE THIN-BAND PROBLEM

### The facts [Tested — Lane 4]

- 18+ holds **15 of 131 items (11%)**: Math 3, Physics 3, Chemistry 3, General Knowledge 2, Social Studies 3, Journals 1 — Lane 4 §(a) [Tested].
- Thinnest cells: Journals 18+ = **1** (`people-places:aconcagua-height` — "a band with 1 case replays the same case every visit"); GK 18+ = **2** — Lane 4 §(d) [Inferred, from Tested counts].
- Math is the most lopsided subject: 22 / 28 / **3** across its bands — 18+ is 5.7% of the largest subject (53 items) — Lane 4 §(d) [Inferred].
- No cell is empty, so no picker row shows "Coming soon" on counts alone — Lane 4 §(d) [Inferred]. The thinness is a *depth* failure, not a visibility failure: the shelf looks stocked; the second visit exposes it.
- Adjacent constraint [Inferred — Lane 4 §(d)]: the existing middle band `10–18` (60 items) has no per-item grade granularity, so splitting it into T2 + T3 already requires re-tagging 60 items. T4 build planning must not assume crew capacity is free.

### Ruling [Inferred]

**T4 shall be a deliberately smaller application layer — a complete *decision curriculum*, not a parallel curriculum. Target shape: 4–6 cases per category (24–36 total), i.e., roughly double today's 15, explicitly NOT parity with T1 (56) or the current 10–18 band (60).**

Reasoning:
1. **There is no sequence to parallel.** A full parallel curriculum implies a topic ladder comparable to T1–T3. For T4 no such ladder exists in any lane (Lane 1 §5c; Lane 2 §6.1; Lane 3 coverage summary). Inventing 50+ topics to match T1's count would manufacture a standard the evidence says does not exist.
2. **Adult value is coverage of decisions, not coverage of topics.** [Inferred] A renter needs the deposit/interest/heat-bill cluster *complete* more than she needs one case each in forty topics. Four to six cases per category, chosen to close a life-domain cluster, deliver a finished experience; fifteen cases scattered to mimic a syllabus deliver a demo.
3. **The 1- and 2-item cells are defects regardless of philosophy.** [Inferred] Journals at 1 and GK at 2 cannot sustain the picker promise ("Pick a subject · Ages 18+" — Lane 4 §(c) [Tested]) because replay collapses to repetition. The minimum viable cell is the §5 set: **4 per category**. Anything below 4 in a released band should either be built to 4 or the band should not be offered for that subject.

### Build-order implications [Inferred]

1. **First: close the sub-viable cells to 4** — Journals (+3) and General Knowledge (+2) — because they are the only cells where a single visit exhausts the band. The §5 prompts are written to be that build list.
2. **Second: build the money cluster across Math + Social Studies together** (interest, credit cost, tax, insurance) — highest-frequency adult decisions, and the cross-category sharing must be designed once, not duplicated twice (Lane 2 D3 separability consequence [Inferred]).
3. **Third: home-and-body cluster** — Physics (energy bills, ramps, driving) and Chemistry (concentration, dilution, labels), extending the inventoried 18+ items rather than replacing them (reuse flags, §5).
4. **Do not start T4 expansion until the T2/T3 re-tag of the 60-item `10–18` band is scheduled** [Inferred] — the same records pipeline serves both, and T4 items written against the old 3-band `AgeGroup` type (`"7-10" | "10-18" | "18+"` — Lane 4, Registry [Tested]) will need the same migration. Sequence the data work once.
5. **Journals scene production is on the critical path** [Inferred from Tested facts, Lane 4 §(c)]: new T4 Journals cases need clue scenes the subject has never had; schedule the media work with the writing, not after it.

## 4. BLOAT CUTS / ANTI-BLOAT — WHAT T4 MUST REFUSE

All cuts are [Inferred] design rulings; each names the evidence pressure behind it.

1. **Refuse to re-teach the K–12 sequence.** No T4 case whose hook is a school topic ("Why do we learn quadratic equations?") without an adult decision attached. Lane 1 §5a: T4 starts *at* the ACT floor; it does not re-teach T3 content labelled "college" [Inferred in Lane 1]. Missing tools are taught inline, in-case, in minutes — never as prerequisite courses.
2. **Refuse AP exam preparation.** No timed exam drills, no CED-shaped unit weights (which are in any case **Data not available** — Lane 1 §8). AP is a side-door on-ramp for credit-seekers (§1), not a content model.
3. **Refuse sub-banding inside T4.** No "18–25 / 26–40 / 40+" ladders and no per-year sequence. No source in the pack supports adult sub-bands (**Data not available** — Lane 1 §5c; Lane 2 §6.1), and sub-banding is how a 24-case layer inflates into a 100-case programme. Differentiation inside T4 is by *domain entry point* (money / home / health / work / citizenship / travel), not by age slice.
4. **Refuse depth past the decision.** No calculus-based physics in the core (AP Physics C track only — Lane 1 §4c [Inferred]); no rote stoichiometry / limiting-reactant sets (beyond the NGSS HS-PS1-7 boundary — Lane 1 §2e [Moderate 1/1]); no mole drills. If a case cannot name the decision its formalism serves, the formalism is cut.
5. **Refuse duplicate ownership.** One case, one home category. Interest lives as a *tool* case in Math and an *institution* case in Social Studies — two different cases, cross-linked, never the same content restyled twice. (This is also the pack's geography lesson: split housing without an assignment rule produces drift — Lane 1 §6.5 [Inferred].)
6. **Refuse trivia-shaped General Knowledge.** GK's existing 18+ items are measurement-flavoured (`great-circle`, `falls-flow`), which is the right instinct. No "capitals and dates" cases at T4; every GK case must end in a travel, property, or news judgement (§2).
7. **Refuse medical, legal, and financial *advice*.** T4 teaches the literacy to read a dosage concentration, a lease clause's arithmetic, and an APR disclosure — and to know when the decision needs a professional. Cases end at *informed judgement and the right question to ask*, never at a recommendation for the learner's own body, contract, or portfolio. [Inferred — scope/ethics ruling of this seat; no pack source governs it, and none is claimed.]
8. **Refuse a separate T4 Journals topic ladder.** Journals rises as a process layer across the other five categories (Lane 1 §6.4; Lane 2 §4 — both [Inferred]). Its 4 T4 cases (§5) are decision records drawn from the other categories' domains, not a seventh syllabus.
9. **Refuse silent reuse.** Where a T4 case extends an inventoried item, the build record says so (§5 reuse flags). Reuse is a virtue — the inventoried 18+ items are the only T4 content with any production history — but unflagged reuse hides how thin the new writing actually is.

## 5. CASE-FILE PROMPTS — T4 COMPLETE

**Format:** Hook question (Meridian style: "Why Do We Learn This?" case files — Lane 4 §(c) [Tested]) + real-world situation + concept taught. **All 24 prompts are [Inferred]** — new design proposals, not sourced standards. **Reuse key:** `REUSE` = extends a named inventoried 18+ item (Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]); `BRIDGE` = builds on an inventoried 10–18 item named in Lane 4 §(b) [Tested]; `NEW` = no inventoried antecedent.

### Math (money, work, measurement)

1. **"Why Do We Learn Percentages?"** — Situation: two job offers, one with a higher salary in a higher-tax, higher-rent city. — Concept: percent-of-what discipline; comparing net, not gross. `NEW` [Inferred]
2. **"Why Do We Learn Interest?"** — Situation: a credit card balance at a stated APR vs a savings account at a stated APY, same principal, one year. — Concept: compound growth in both directions; total cost vs monthly payment. `NEW` [Inferred] *(Tool half of the Math/Social Studies money cluster, §3.)*
3. **"Why Do We Learn to Split Shares?"** — Situation: three flatmates splitting rent, a deposit, and a shared car repair in unequal proportions they must defend to each other. — Concept: combining and comparing fractional/proportional shares. `REUSE` — extends `math-number:fractions:combine-shares` (18+) [Inferred]
4. **"Why Do We Learn to Check a Measurement?"** — Situation: a contractor's quote for flooring is priced by area; the learner measures the room and finds the quote's area is a third too large. — Concept: area, unit consistency, and estimation as fraud protection. `REUSE` — extends `math-applied:measure:survey-check` (18+); estimation stance also extends `math-number:tables:mental-strategies` (18+) [Inferred]

### Physics (home, travel, work)

5. **"Why Do We Learn About Energy?"** — Situation: a winter electricity bill doubles; the learner must find which appliance, run for how long, explains it. — Concept: energy = power × time; the kWh as a purchased unit; accounting for a "missing" quantity. `REUSE` — extends `physics:energy:missing` (18+) [Inferred]
6. **"Why Do We Learn About Speed and Stopping?"** — Situation: choosing a safe following distance and understanding why a small speed increase sharply lengthens braking distance. — Concept: kinetic energy scaling with the square of speed (T2 endpoint, MS-PS3 — Lane 1 §2e [Moderate 1/1]) applied to a driving decision; time–distance reasoning. `REUSE` — extends `physics:arrive:adult-time` (18+) [Inferred]
7. **"Why Do We Learn About Slope?"** — Situation: building a wheelchair-accessible ramp (or a moving ramp for furniture) that is legal, buildable, and not dangerously steep. — Concept: ratio as rise-over-run; slope as a standard you can measure and check. `REUSE` — extends `physics:ramp:adult-ratio` (18+) [Inferred]
8. **"Why Do We Learn About Levers?"** — Situation: moving a heavy appliance alone with a dolly, a plank, and a fulcrum — or choosing where to stand with a breaker bar on a seized bolt. — Concept: mechanical advantage; trading distance for force. `BRIDGE` — builds on `physics:lever:seesaw` (7–10) re-entered at adult load [Inferred]

### Chemistry (health, home, food)

9. **"Why Do We Learn About Concentration?"** — Situation: diluting a cleaning concentrate from its label ratio, and recognising why "double-strength" is a hazard, not a bargain. — Concept: concentration as amount per volume; dilution ratios. `NEW` [Inferred] *(Label literacy only — §4, cut 7.)*
10. **"Why Do We Learn About Reaction Rates?"** — Situation: food spoiling faster in a warm kitchen, and why refrigeration, sealing, and acidity each slow a different clock. — Concept: rate dependence on temperature, surface, and concentration. `REUSE` — extends `chemistry:reactions:rate` (18+) [Inferred]
11. **"Why Do We Learn That Mass Is Conserved?"** — Situation: a recipe scaled by weight, a fizzy reaction in a sealed vs open bottle, and a product claim that something "disappeared." — Concept: conservation of mass as an audit tool for everyday claims. `REUSE` — extends `chemistry:reactions:mass` (18+) [Inferred]
12. **"Why Do We Learn to Explain a Change?"** — Situation: rust on a bike left outside, tarnish, a cut apple browning — the learner must explain one change as a new substance forming, not a substance "wearing out." — Concept: chemical vs physical change, evidenced explanation. `REUSE` — extends `chemistry:reactions:explain` (18+); also builds on `chemistry:reactions:rust` (10–18) [Inferred]

### General Knowledge — Geography / Earth & Space / Interdisciplinary (travel, place, planet, health-systems)

13. **"Why Do We Learn About Great Circles?"** — Situation: a flight from North America to Asia arcs over the Arctic on the booking map, and a flat map says the "straight" route is shorter. — Concept: shortest path on a sphere vs straight line on a projection. `REUSE` — extends `general-knowledge:mixed:great-circle` (18+) [Inferred]
14. **"Why Do We Learn About Rivers and Flow?"** — Situation: choosing whether a house near a river is a bargain or a flood risk, using flow, catchment, and a flood map. — Concept: flow rate, watersheds, and hazard as a system (ESS3, Earth and Human Activity — Lane 1 §2b [Moderate 1/1]). `REUSE` — extends `general-knowledge:mixed:falls-flow` (18+) [Inferred]
15. **"Why Do We Learn About Time Zones?"** — Situation: scheduling a call and a medication schedule across a long-haul trip so neither the meeting nor the dose lands at 3 a.m. body time. — Concept: longitudinal time arithmetic; elapsed time across zones. `BRIDGE` — builds on `social-studies:compare:time-zones` (10–18), housed here as travel GK [Inferred]
16. **"Why Do We Learn About the Body's Systems?"** — Situation: reading a basic blood-panel result — a value, a reference range, and a "high" flag — and knowing what kind of question to bring to a clinician. — Concept: the body as regulated systems (feedback, ranges, rates); health numeracy. `NEW` [Inferred] *(The bounded Life Science strand — see §6. Literacy only, never advice — §4, cut 7.)*

### Social Studies — History / Civics / Economics / Human Geography (citizenship, money, travel)

17. **"Why Do We Learn About Interest and Credit?"** — Situation: two loan offers for the same car — lower monthly payment, longer term, higher total cost. — Concept: how lending institutions price time and risk; total cost of credit. `NEW` [Inferred] *(Institution half of the money cluster; Math #2 owns the tool — §4, cut 5.)*
18. **"Why Do We Learn About Taxes?"** — Situation: a first pay stub (or a freelancer's first quarterly bill) where the gross-to-net gap must be explained line by line. — Concept: marginal vs effective rates; what each deduction funds. `NEW` [Inferred]
19. **"Why Do We Learn How Laws Are Made?"** — Situation: a local ballot measure / council proposal the learner must vote on, with cost claims from both campaigns. — Concept: C3 civic processes at adult stakes — who decides, who pays, who benefits (D2.Eco.1.9-12 framing — Lane 1 §3a [Low 1/1]); evaluating a policy's costs/benefits across groups. `NEW` [Inferred]

## 6. AGREEMENTS & DISSENTS

### Agreements

- **Agree — Lane 1 §5c / §8:** no national T4 topic standard exists; any council product that presents T4 topics as standards-derived is misrepresenting the evidence. T4 must be published as a Meridian design, floor-checked against ACT [Moderate 1/1] and orientation-checked against AAC&U [Low 1/1], nothing more.
- **Agree — Lane 1 §6.4 and Lane 2 §4 (both [Inferred]):** Journals is a process/practice layer, not a content subject. This memo's §2 and §4 (cut 8) adopt that position wholesale.
- **Agree — Lane 2 §5 D3 design consequence [Inferred]:** Economics and Personal Finance must be separable units. Extended here to the Math ↔ Social Studies money cluster (§3, build order 2).
- **Agree — Lane 4 §(d) [Inferred]:** the `10–18` band cannot be split into T2/T3 from existing records; the re-tag of 60 items is real work and gates T4 scheduling (§3, build order 4).
- **Agree — Lane 1 §2e anchors [Moderate 1/1]:** quantification is the STEM difficulty dial, and the T3 ceilings (F = ma quantified; mole as conversion) cap the T4 core. T4's move is *sideways into decisions*, not *upward into formalism*.

### Dissents

- **Dissent (partial) — from a literal reading of Lane 1 §5a's implication** that T4 "should start at that assumed floor and build general-education depth above it" [Inferred in Lane 1]. If "above the floor" is read as *sequence* (first the floor's subjects, then depth), this seat dissents: adult entry is problem-first, and the floor functions as a diagnostic assumption inside cases, not as a starting syllabus (§1). The floor values themselves are not disputed [Moderate 1/1].
- **Dissent — from treating AP as T4 content in any form stronger than "bridge."** Lane 1 §5d lists AP as "the practical bridge," which this memo accepts; any stronger claim (T4 = AP topics continued) is rejected in §1, Option B — and is in any case unbuildable on this pack, since CED detail is **Data not available** (Lane 1 §4d, §8).
- **Dissent — from parity as a quality signal.** Any council position that T4 should match T1/T2 item counts to look "complete" is rejected in §3: on this evidence, count parity would require inventing a standard. A finished 24-case decision layer outranks an unfinished 56-case syllabus.

### Life Science position — this seat's ruling [Inferred]

**Question:** Adults meet biology as health. Does that change the housing question?

**Answer: Yes — for T4 specifically, it does.**

- The gap itself is evidenced three times over: LS1–LS4 is a full NGSS discipline with no Meridian home (Lane 1 §2f [Moderate 1/1 for the DCI list]); the ACT Science benchmark course is *Biology* (23) — Lane 1 §5a [Moderate 1/1]; AP Biology exists with no Meridian home — Lane 1 §4b [Moderate 1/1]. Lane 2 adds that Texas names Biology as a standalone HS course and graduation science credit — Lane 2 §1a [High 2/2].
- For T1–T3 this seat takes no position on the add-a-category vs house-in-GK vs out-of-scope choice (Lane 1 §2f lists the options and takes no position; neither does this memo, for those tiers).
- **For T4, the ruling is different [Inferred]:** an adult tier that teaches interest, energy bills, and ballot measures but offers nothing on reading a lab range, a dosage concentration, or a risk claim has excluded the domain where adults most often face a consequential, numeracy-laden decision involving science. The motivational premise is decisive here in a way it is not for children: the adult's body is not a future application of biology; it is the present one.
- **Housing [Inferred]:** do **not** create a seventh T4 category, and do **not** wait for the T1–T3 Life Science decision. House a **bounded applied-health strand** inside the existing structure: GK–Interdisciplinary carries the systems/concepts cases (prompt #16), Journals carries the observation/record cases (prompt #24), and Chemistry carries concentration/dilution literacy (prompt #9). Three to four cases total, explicitly scoped as *health literacy* (read, judge, ask) under §4 cut 7 (never advice). If the owner later rules Life Science a full category for all tiers, this strand folds into it without waste; if the owner rules Life Science out of scope, this strand is the documented, deliberate exception for adults — recorded as an exception, not smuggled in as a category.
- **What is NOT claimed:** that any standard requires this strand. No lane evidences an adult health-literacy curriculum for Meridian — **Data not available**. The strand is justified as an [Inferred] design judgement from the ACT Biology benchmark's existence [Moderate 1/1] plus the adult-decision premise of the council charge, and it should be presented to learners and reviewers exactly that way.
