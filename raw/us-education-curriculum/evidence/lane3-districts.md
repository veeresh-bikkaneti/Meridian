## 1. NYC Public Schools (NYCPS) — official documents YIELDED

Official portals reached: schools.nyc.gov (Subjects, NYC Solves), WeTeachNYC (NYCPS's own teacher resource library, hosts the official Scope & Sequence collections), plus an NYCPS school site reproducing the Passport unit list verbatim.

### (a) Math sequence
- **System structure:** "NYC Solves" standardises math citywide — all high schools use **Illustrative Mathematics**; middle-school districts choose from a pre-approved list; the first phase focused on middle school + 9th-grade Algebra, later phases extend to remaining HS grades and elementary. https://stg.schools.nyc.gov/learning/subjects/math/nyc-solves [Moderate 1/1 — 1 primary]
- **T3 (Gr 9–12):** By end of 2024-25 the majority of high schools were using Illustrative Mathematics **for Algebra 1**; NYC Solves "will require high school math classrooms to adopt a single, uniform curriculum called Illustrative Mathematics." Full HS course-by-course order (Algebra 1 → Geometry → Algebra 2 → Pre-Calc) is **not stated on the official pages fetched** — Data not available beyond Algebra 1 anchoring grade 9. https://www.schools.nyc.gov/about-us/news/announcements/2024/06/24/mayor-adams-chancellor-banks-unveil-more-equitable-vision-for-future-of-new-york-city-public-schools-with-signature-math-initiative-and-new-division-of-inclusive-and-accessible-learning [Moderate 1/1]
- **T2 (Gr 6–8):** 93 middle schools in 8 districts used Illustrative Math from fall 2024 under NYC Solves; districts choose from pre-approved curricula (not one mandated book). Grade-level topic sequence inside those curricula: Data not available from NYCPS pages fetched. Same source as above. [Moderate 1/1]
- **T1 (Gr 2–5):** NYC Solves originally excluded elementary ("Later phases will extend to … elementary"); a later mayoral announcement expands NYC Solves into grades K-5 with **district-chosen single curricula** — named examples: District 5 i-Ready Mathematics Classroom; Districts 11/12 Illustrative Mathematics; District 25 Amplify Desmos Mathematics. So there is **no single citywide elementary math sequence** — it varies by community district [Inferred from the district-by-district list]. https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/05/mayor-mamdani-and-chancellor-samuelsannounce-expansion-of-nyc-so [Moderate 1/1]

### (b) Science sequence
- **Official document exists:** "The New York City PK-8 Science Scope and Sequence 2022" — updated scope & sequence aligned to the NYS P-12 Science Learning Standards (based on NGSS), covering all science units Pre-K–grade 8, with year-long and unit overviews by grade; the collection also covers grades 9-12 plus **all Regents science courses**. https://www.weteachnyc.org/resources/collection/scope-and-sequence-science/ [Moderate 1/1 — 1 primary]
- **T1 (Gr 2–5) — grade 2 example (via IXL's alignment to the NYC DOE Scope & Sequence, secondary mirror of the official document):** Gr 2 units = (1) Properties and Patterns of Water (matter properties, heating/cooling changes — early **chemistry/physical**), (2) Changes to Land Over Time (**earth science**: land/water shapes, erosion), (3) Plant and Animal Interactions (life science). Pattern [Inferred]: each elementary grade mixes physical + earth + life units rather than teaching one discipline per year. https://www.ixl.com/science/skill-plans/nyc-doe-scope-and-sequence-grade-2 [Low 1/1 — 1 secondary]
- **T2 (Gr 6–8):** NYC's SCALE Science 6-8 "follows an **integrated model** in which earth and space science, life science, and physical science are intertwined within each unit of study" — i.e., physics/chemistry/earth topics are **distributed across all three middle grades**, not blocked by year. Documented unit anchors include 6:2 Weather and the Atmosphere (earth), 6:3 Diversity of Life / 6:4 Interdependence (life), 8:2 Forces and Motion on Earth (physics), 8:3 The Sun, Earth and Moon System (earth/space), 7:2 Energy and Matter (physical/chemistry-adjacent). https://www.weteachnyc.org/resources/resource/scale-science-6-8/ [Moderate 1/1]
- **T3 (Gr 9–12):** Regents-course structure: **Living Environment** (biology — documented units at grade 9 level, e.g. characteristics of living things, nutrients/energy/biochemical processes), **Earth Science** (documented units: Earth's interior & plate tectonics; landscapes & surface processes; Earth-sun-moon system), plus Chemistry and Physics as Regents courses in the 9-12 scope collection. Exact grade placement of Chemistry vs Physics varies by school — Data not available as a single mandated NYCPS order. https://www.weteachnyc.org/resources/collection/scope-and-sequence-science/ and https://www.weteachnyc.org/resources/resource/earth-science-earths-interior-plate-tectonics/?collection_id=753 [Moderate 2/2]

### (c) Social studies sequence
NYCPS's named curriculum is **Passport to Social Studies** (K-12), built on the NYS Social Studies Framework; companion programme **Civics for All**. https://www.weteachnyc.org/resources/resource/social-studies-scope-and-sequence-grades-9-12/?collection_id=87 [Moderate 1/1]
- **T1 Passport units (school-site verbatim list of the official curriculum):**
  - Gr 2: Rights, Rules & Responsibilities → NYC Over Time → Urban/Suburban/Rural Communities → Our Community's Geography
  - Gr 3: Introduction to World Geography & World Communities (case studies: China, Nigeria, Egypt, Peru)
  - Gr 4: Geography of New York State → Native Americans of NYS → Colonial & Revolutionary Periods → Government (federal/state/local) → Immigration/Industrialization/Westward Movement
  - Gr 5: Geography & Early Societies of the Western Hemisphere → European Exploration → Comparative Western Hemisphere case studies (Mexico, US, Dominican Republic, Canada) → Western Hemisphere Today
  https://www.ps255.org/social_studies [Moderate 1/1 — official NYCPS school site reproducing the district curriculum]
- **Framework cross-check (state framework NYCPS implements):** Gr 3 Communities Around the World; Gr 4 Local History & Local Government; Gr 5 Western Hemisphere; Gr 6 Eastern Hemisphere; Gr 7 US & NY History I; Gr 8 US & NY History II; Gr 9 Global History & Geography I; Gr 10 Global History & Geography II; Gr 11 US History & Government; Gr 12 Participation in Government (+ Economics). https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/ss-framework-k-12-intro.pdf [Moderate 1/1 — state primary; NYCPS alignment stated on WeTeachNYC]
- **T3 Passport detail (NYCPS school site):** Gr 9 Global I (Paleolithic → classical civilisations, 8 units); Gr 10 Global II (1750 → present, Regents-aligned); Gr 11 US History; Gr 12 Participation in Government + Economics (Economics units: Personal Finance, Markets, Indicators). https://www.cobblehillhighschool.org/social-studies [Low 1/1 — 1 school-site secondary for the district curriculum]

### (d) Distinctive
- **Civics for All** runs as a companion strand across 9-12 alongside Passport. Source: WeTeachNYC 9-12 collection above. [Moderate 1/1]
- Curriculum is **not uniform below high school**: community districts pick from approved lists (math) — a "menu within guardrails" model [Inferred]. Sources: NYC Solves pages above.
- **MOSAIC / Black Studies:** NYCPS's Black Studies PK-12 curriculum framework is explicitly aligned to Passport's scope & sequence (K-8 and 9-12) — an overlay, not a separate sequence. https://www.tc.columbia.edu/berc/curriculum/ [Low 1/1 — university partner secondary]

## 2. LAUSD — official documents YIELDED (structure strong, unit content behind Drive links)

Official portal reached: LAUSD's Equitable Grading & Instruction (EGI) site (lausd.org subdomain) publishing **Curriculum Maps for every grade/subject**, plus the district A-G policy bulletin on lausd.net. The maps themselves sit behind Google Drive links whose contents were not readable in this pass — so LAUSD yields the **course/map architecture** with high confidence, but unit-level topic order inside each map = Data not available.

### (a) Math sequence (from the official EGI Curriculum Maps index)
- **T1 (Gr 2–5):** "Math EM" (Eureka Math) curriculum map per grade — TK, K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 each has its own EM map. https://egi.lausd.org/apps/pages/index.jsp?uREC_ID=4409176&type=d&pREC_ID=2696485 [Moderate 1/1]
- **T2 (Gr 6–8):** Math Sixth Grade; Math Seventh Grade; Math Eighth Grade maps, plus acceleration maps: Highly Accelerated Math 6/7; Accelerated 7th; Highly Accelerated 8th = **Algebra 1**; Accel Algebra 1 (8th). So the documented default is grade-level math 6→7→8 with an official accelerated lane reaching Algebra 1 in grade 8. Same EGI source. [Moderate 1/1]
- **T3 (Gr 9–12):** Maps named by course in grade order: **09 Math Algebra 1 → 10 Math Geometry → 11 Math Algebra 2 → 12 Math Pre-Calculus**, plus 12 Math Stats/Probability and 11.1 Honors Advanced Math. Same EGI source. [Moderate 1/1]
- A-G bulletin corroboration (older, 2006 — treat as historical policy, not current pacing): Gr 9 math = Algebra 1 / Geometry / Algebra 2; Gr 8 = Algebra 1 (or Algebra Readiness). https://www.lausd.net/math/Memos&Bulletins/BUL-2513.1_AG_REQ.pdf [Low 1/1 — dated 2006]

### (b) Science sequence
- **T1 (Gr 2–5):** Two parallel map sets per grade: "Amplify Science [grade]" and "Science [grade]" maps for K-5 — i.e., Amplify Science is a documented elementary adoption. EGI source above. [Moderate 1/1]
- **T2 (Gr 6–8):** **Three parallel curriculum-map options per grade** — HMH Science, Amplify Science, and Stanford SCALE maps for each of grades 6, 7, 8 (schools use one of the three). All are NGSS middle-school programmes [Inferred: integrated life/physical/earth per grade rather than one discipline per year — the map titles are by grade, not by discipline]. EGI source above. [Moderate 1/1 for existence; integration = Inferred]
- **T3 (Gr 9–12):** HS science maps are the **California three-course NGSS model**, by discipline-in-earth-system framing: **Biology of Living Earth; Chemistry in the Earth System; Physics of the Universe** — offered in three publisher versions (Savvas/Pearson, HMH, Discovery Education). This is decisive for Meridian: in LAUSD's documented HS model, "physics" and "chemistry" are **not standalone silos** — each is taught integrated with earth/space science. EGI source above. [Moderate 1/1]
- Older A-G bulletin named "Integrated Coordinated Science I/II" as the Gr 9-10 science option alongside Biology/Physics/Chemistry — superseded framing vs the current EGI three-course titles; noted as conflict/history. https://www.lausd.net/math/Memos&Bulletins/BUL-2513.1_AG_REQ.pdf [Low 1/1]

### (c) Social studies (History-Social Science, "HSS")
- **T1/T2:** HSS maps per grade K-8 on the EGI index (HSS Kindergarten … HSS Eighth Grade). Content titles inside maps: Data not available (Drive-gated). EGI source above. [Moderate 1/1 for existence]
- **T2 course titles (2006 A-G bulletin):** Gr 6 World History & Geography: Ancient Civilizations; Gr 7 World History & Geography: Medieval to Modern Times; Gr 8 United States History: Growth and Conflict. https://www.lausd.net/math/Memos&Bulletins/BUL-2513.1_AG_REQ.pdf [Low 1/1 — dated; matches the California HSS framework pattern, but not re-verified in a current LAUSD document in this pass]
- **T3:** EGI HS maps list only: 10 HSS Tenth Grade; 11 HSS Eleventh Grade; 12 HSS Twelfth Grade — **ECON** and **GOV** as separate maps. No 9th-grade HSS map is listed (grade 9 HSS is elective/Ethnic Studies territory in CA [Inferred]). A-G pattern from school sites on lausd.org: Gr 10 World History, Gr 11 US History, Gr 12 Principles of American Democracy + Economics. https://bravomedhs.lausd.org/apps/pages/index.jsp?uREC_ID=3800757&type=d&pREC_ID=2460599 [Low 1/1 — school-site sample schedule]

### (d) Distinctive
- **A-G as graduation requirement:** LAUSD requires the full UC/CSU A-G 15-course college-prep sequence to graduate (Board resolution 2005, implemented from fall 2008) — the most college-bound default of the five districts. https://www.lausd.net/math/Memos&Bulletins/BUL-2513.1_AG_REQ.pdf [Moderate 1/1 — policy bulletin; age noted]
- **Choice architecture:** unlike NYC/CPS single-curriculum pushes, LAUSD's official index documents **multiple approved programmes per subject/band** (3 science options in MS; 3 publishers for HS science) [Inferred from EGI index]. EGI source above.

## 3. Chicago Public Schools (CPS) — official documents YIELDED (strongest HS documentation of the five)

Official portals reached: cps.edu Skyline curriculum pages (per-grade/per-course), CPS Graduation Requirements page + Board policy 605.3, CPS-hosted Skyline scope & sequence PDFs (volta.cps.edu).

### (a) Math sequence
- **Skyline Mathematics** is the district curriculum PreK-12, launched July 2021 with full scopes & sequences, unit plans and assessments in ELA, Math, Science, Social Science, Spanish, French. https://www.cps.edu/sites/skyline/curriculum/mathematics/ and https://www.cps.edu/sites/blog/search/2021/june/five-things-to-know-about-skyline/ [High 2/2 — 2 agreeing primary pages]
- **T1/T2 topic-level unit lists:** Skyline per-grade math pages exist, but unit/topic titles for grades 2-8 were not extractable from the official pages fetched — **Data not available** at topic level (existence of grade-level Skyline math for every grade K-8 is documented via CPS Curriculum Quality tables rating Skyline Math "High Quality" for K-2, 3-5, 6-8). https://www.cps.edu/schools/profiles/curriculum-and-instruction/curriculum-quality/609903 [Moderate 1/1]
- **T3 (official Sample Course of Study):** Gr 9 **Algebra** → Gr 10 **Geometry** → Gr 11 **Advanced Algebra/Trigonometry** → Gr 12 Pre-Calculus / AP Calculus (elective examples). Graduation requires exactly those first three (1.0 credit each); students who earned Algebra credit early still owe 3 HS math credits (Geometry, Adv Algebra/Trig, + advanced math). https://www.cps.edu/academics/graduation-requirements [Moderate 1/1 — 1 primary, detailed]

### (b) Science sequence
- **T3 — the headline:** CPS Board policy requires, for freshmen entering 2018 or later, **1.0 credit Biology + 1.0 credit Chemistry + 1.0 credit Physics for every graduate** — all three lab sciences are compulsory, not electives. Sample order: Gr 9 Biology → Gr 10 Chemistry → Gr 11 Physics → Gr 12 electives (Environmental Science / AP Biology / AP Chemistry / dual enrollment). https://www.cps.edu/sites/cps-policy-rules/policies/600/605/605-3/ and https://www.cps.edu/academics/graduation-requirements [High 2/2]
- **T1/T2:** Skyline Science K-8 is phenomenon-based, three-dimensional (NGSS-style): K-5 units end in performance-based assessments; 6-8 units end in Socratic Seminar + end-of-unit assessment; grades 6-8 are required to receive 300 minutes of science/week and take the Illinois Science Assessment in grade 8. Discipline mix per the CPS STEM family guide: life, physical and earth science are all present across 6-8 (integrated, not blocked by year) [Inferred from guide wording]. https://www.cps.edu/sites/skyline/curriculum/science/ and https://www.cpsstem.cps.edu/ourpages/auto/2023/3/23/62116602/STEM_1-Pager_7_6-8-Science-Guide_647266_EN_V8.pdf?rnd=1694469958029 [Moderate 2/2]
- Note: CPS Curriculum Quality table lists **Amplify Science** (not Skyline) as the 6-8 science curriculum in at least the profiled programme — i.e., middle-school science adoption may vary by school/network; Skyline Science pages describe the district curriculum offer. Flagged as a partial conflict. https://www.cps.edu/schools/profiles/curriculum-and-instruction/curriculum-quality/609903 [Low 1/1]

### (c) Social studies ("Social Science" in CPS naming)
- **T1:** IMPACT/Skyline Social Science per grade. Documented: Gr 2 course title **"Families, Neighborhoods, and Communities"** — inquiry through history, geography, economy and government lenses; Unit 1 "Today and Long Ago." https://www.volta.cps.edu/ourpages/auto/2026/8/18/56418477/CPS%20Scope%20and%20Sequence%20-%20Grade%202%20Social%20Science%20.pdf [Moderate 1/1]
- **T1 upper:** Gr 5 course **"Our Nation, Our World"** — American history, government, cultures from first Native peoples through the late 1800s; 8 units incl. Global Movement & Connections, Native Americans in North America, Race/Power/Identity in Colonial North America, Road to War, American Revolution, The Constitution, A Nation Goes to War Over Slavery, Pains & Promises in a Divided Nation. https://www.volta.cps.edu/ourpages/auto/2025/8/11/46336645/G5 Curriculum Map - Google Docs.pdf [Moderate 1/1]
- **T2:** Gr 6 **World History** — "ancient, medieval and early modern world history from the beginning of civilization to the age of exploration" (units incl. Identity & Community; Roots & Threads of Civilization; Rise, Expansion & Fall of Empires). CPS PD listings place **Civics at grades 8 (& 11)** in the Skyline social-science banding ("Social Science Civics 8, 11"; "Social Science 6, 7, 9, 10"). https://www.cps.edu/sites/skyline/curriculum/social-science/grade-6/ and https://mailchi.mp/cps.edu/skyline-sprints-happening-now-reserve-your-spot-17586667 [Moderate 2/2]
- **T3 (official):** Graduation: World Studies 1.0 + US History 1.0 + Civics/AP Gov 0.5 + other Social Science 0.5. Sample order: Gr 9 World Studies → Gr 10 US History → Gr 11 Civics (0.5) → Gr 12 AP options. https://www.cps.edu/academics/graduation-requirements [Moderate 1/1]

### (d) Distinctive
- **Financial Education is a graduation requirement** (non-credit-bearing): every student must complete a course integrating a Financial Education unit (policy renamed from Consumer Ed for the class entering 2017+). Same CPS page/policy above. [High 2/2]
- **Computer Science 1.0 credit required** for graduation (recommended grade 9) — unique among the five districts documented here. Same source. [Moderate 1/1]
- Skyline itself: first-ever district-built, culturally responsive, freely available PreK-12 curriculum (Curriculum Equity Initiative, 2021). https://www.cps.edu/sites/blog/search/2021/june/five-things-to-know-about-skyline/ [Moderate 1/1]

## 4. Miami-Dade County Public Schools (M-DCPS) — official documents PARTIALLY yielded

M-DCPS publishes District Pacing Guides for every grade/course, but the guides live behind the employee portal / Learning Village; publicly reachable copies are **mirrors of official M-DCPS documents** on school and third-party hosts (tagged accordingly). The Division of Academics site (dadeschools.net) confirms the current apparatus (District Curriculum Bulletin, Student Progression Plan, pacing-guide system) without exposing guide contents. https://divisionofacademics.dadeschools.net/ [Moderate 1/1 for the system's existence]

### (a) Math sequence
- **T3 recommended sequence (M-DCPS school-hosted Curriculum Bulletin extract):** Algebra I by end of Gr 9 → Geometry by end of Gr 10 → a course ≥ Algebra I by Gr 11; four-year recommended sequence = **Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, Pre-Calculus**. https://mkhs.dadeschools.net/Curriculum%20Files/Course%20Req.htm [Low 1/1 — official-district school host, dated document]
- **Current Florida standard (applies to M-DCPS):** 24-credit diploma requires **4 math credits including Algebra 1 (EOC must be passed) and Geometry**. https://www.floridacapprep.com/blog/florida-high-school-graduation-requirements-explained [Low 1/1 — secondary summary of state statute]
- **T1/T2 topic sequence:** M-DCPS math pacing guides exist per grade (per the district's K-12 Comprehensive Plan description) but no public official copy was reachable — **Data not available** at topic level. Plan description: https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/32744120/science-plan-miami-dade-county-public-schools/9 [Low 1/1]

### (b) Science sequence
- **Structure (district's own K-12 Comprehensive Science Plan, mirrored):** Science pacing guides are organised by four Bodies of Knowledge — **Nature of Science; Earth & Space Science; Life Science; Physical Science** — across K-5 and 6-8 (incl. MS honors), with HS guides for **Physical Science, Biology, Chemistry** (and Physics in the recommended sequence). I.e., elementary/middle science is **integrated across all four BOKs every year**, discipline courses start in HS. Same Yumpu mirror above. [Low 1/1 — mirrored official document]
- **T3 recommended sequence (school-hosted bulletin extract):** **Earth/Space Science by end of Gr 9 → Biology I by end of Gr 10 → Chemistry I or Physical Science by end of Gr 11**; four-year recommended = Earth/Space, Biology I, Chemistry I, **Physics I**. Advanced track: Earth/Space in Gr 8 → Biology Gr 9 → Physical Science/Chemistry Gr 10. https://mkhs.dadeschools.net/Curriculum%20Files/Course%20Req.htm [Low 1/1]
- A second M-DCPS bulletin extract states the 3-credit science core as "Physical Science, Biology 1, Chemistry" + one of Physics / Anatomy & Physiology / Marine Science / Environmental Science — ordering of the first course differs between the two extracts (Earth/Space-first vs Physical-Science-first); **conflict flagged**, both are dated bulletin versions. https://mater-lakes-collegiate.devrelease.net/uploads/new-page-blocks/July2022/3xs1e428YRRKdRGiOHvA.pdf [Low 1/1]

### (c) Social studies sequence
- **System:** District Pacing Guides "outline the required curriculum for social studies, grades K-12," developed for every elementary grade and every required MS/HS course, sequenced in 9-week segments. https://docsbay.net/doc/938250/Miami-Dade-County-Public-Schools-s2 [Low 1/1 — mirrored official guide]
- **T1:** Gr 3 course title documented: **"Third Grade — The United States Regions and Its Neighbors"** (strands: American History, Geography, Economics, Civics). Same mirror above. [Low 1/1]
- **T2:** Gr 6 = **World History** (Course Code 2109010; strands World History, Geography, Civics, Economics; themes incl. geography & people/place/environment relationships). https://doczz.net/doc/8655162/big-idea-%232---department-of-social-sciences---miami [Low 1/1 — mirrored official guide]
- **T2:** Gr 7 = **Civics** (year-at-a-glance: What is Civics → Foundations of Government → Creating the US Constitution → Political Participation → Organisation of the Constitution → Branches of Government → Constitutional Amendments → Impacts of Law → Globalization; built around the **Grade 7 Civics End-of-Course Exam**, whose benchmarks are flagged inside guides from grade 3 up). https://westlabschool.org/ourpages/auto/2019/8/20/54216566/7th%20Grade%20Civics%20Pacing%20Guide%20Year%20at%20a%20Glance.pdf [Moderate 1/1 — M-DCPS school host of the district guide]
- **T2 Gr 8 / T3 social studies course order:** Data not available from reachable official copies in this pass (Florida's standard pattern — Gr 8 US History; HS World History / US History / Gov+Economics — is **not** asserted here because no M-DCPS document stating it was retrieved).

### (d) Distinctive
- **Standalone Civics year in grade 7 with a high-stakes EOC** — civics is a full middle-school course, not a HS semester (contrast CPS/NYC grade 12). Sources above. [Moderate 1/1]
- **Financial literacy:** Florida law requires **0.5 credit Personal Financial Literacy & Money Management** for students entering Gr 9 in 2023-24 or later (class of 2027+), within the 24-credit diploma — applies in M-DCPS. Statute text: https://www.flhouse.gov/Statutes/2023/1003.41/ [Moderate 1/1 — state primary; district application Inferred]
- Pacing guides are explicitly **mandatory**: "to be utilized by all teachers, grades K-12" — the tightest pacing regime documented among the five. Mirror source above. [Low 1/1]

## 5. Houston ISD (HISD) — official scope & sequence NOT publicly reachable

**Verdict: partial failure, stated plainly.** HISD's current public curriculum pages describe the programme's philosophy and subjects but publish **no grade-by-grade scope & sequence**. The district's own (2014) Curriculum Document Access Guide states that Pacing Calendars, Scope & Sequence and Vertical Alignment Matrices were public on the old curriculum site while Curriculum Maps / Unit Planning Guides sat behind the **login-gated eLearn portal**; the current site (redesigned) lists subjects only. https://www.houstonisd.org/schools-academics/academics/curriculum-2/explore-hisd-curriculum and https://silo.tips/download/curriculum-document-access-guide [Moderate 2/2 for the access situation]

### (a) Math sequence
- **Documented on official HISD pages:** curriculum is built on **TEKS** (Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills); subjects include Mathematics, Science, Social Studies + the NES-distinctive **"Art of Thinking."** https://www.houstonisd.org/schools-academics/academics/curriculum-2 [Moderate 1/1]
- **T2 (one official campus page):** an NES middle school documents **Advanced Math in 6th and 7th grades, and Algebra 1 in 8th grade** as its advanced options — evidence of an Algebra-1-by-grade-8 lane, not a districtwide sequence. https://deady.houstonisd.org/academics-programs [Low 1/1]
- **T1 topic sequence / T3 course order:** Data not available from public official HISD documents in this pass. (A TxVSN approved-course list names HISD HS courses — Algebra I, Algebra II, Precalculus, AP Calculus; Biology, Chemistry, Physics; World History/Geography, US History, US Government, AP Human Geography — confirming course *existence*, not sequence.) https://catalog.mytxvsn.org/Documents/HoustonISD-TxVSNApprovedCourses.pdf [Low 1/1]

### (b) Science sequence
- Data not available from public official HISD documents (no published grade placement for physics/chemistry/earth-space topics was reachable). Course existence only: see TxVSN list above. [Low 1/1]

### (c) Social studies sequence
- Data not available from public official HISD documents. (Texas TEKS prescribes a state sequence, but no HISD document stating HISD's placement was retrieved, so it is not asserted here.)

### (d) Distinctive
- **New Education System (NES):** HISD's centrally-written curriculum (launched fall 2023) — lessons are **district-authored, not textbook/pre-made programmes**, built step-by-step (model → practice → mastery) on TEKS, with the extra subject **Art of Thinking** (critical-thinking class) in NES schools. https://www.houstonisd.org/schools-academics/academics/curriculum-2/explore-hisd-curriculum and https://cage.houstonisd.org/our-academics-programs [Moderate 2/2]
- HISD reports pre-NES it lagged the state average on all STAAR assessments; by 2026, 10 of 12 Gr 3-8 math/reading tests met/exceeded the state average — context for why the curriculum is centrally controlled and portal-gated [Inferred]. https://cmartinez.houstonisd.org/all-news [Low 1/1]
