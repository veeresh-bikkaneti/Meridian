## 9.2 Situational Awareness (Pillar 4) - source base + honest scope

- **Verified base:** NHTSA pedestrian/bicycle/teen pages above carry the scanning and hazard-anticipation content (check signs/signals/drivers/cyclists around you; do not rely on others; wait for a complete stop; walking buddy rule) [Moderate 1/2]. The pack's T1 GK "severe-weather preparation" prompt and ESS3 hazard cases (Lane 1, Lane 6 LS2) are the emergency-decision base.
- **Honest limit:** there is no single federal "situational awareness curriculum" for K-12; the audit's 0-hit finding is a scope gap, not a sourcing failure. Pillar 4 is therefore designed as a **protocol layer** (scan → assess → decide → act) layered on the sourced NHTSA/ESS3 content, tagged [Inferred] until an official curriculum source is adopted at authoring time. Do not present it as a sourced standard.
- **Tier mapping [Inferred]:** T1 = recognize hazards in own environment (crossing, parking lot, weather); T2 = scan routines + risk spotting in groups (bike, crowds, online-adjacent); T3 = hazard anticipation while driving/walking (DMV + NHTSA teen); T4 = emergency decision-making (adult decisions: evacuation, first aid triage, financial/legal risk spotting).
