# Lane 11 — NCF-Envirothon & Kansas Envirothon: Environmental Studies Benchmark

Purpose. This lane anchors High School Environmental Studies to the
NCF-Envirothon's official testing categories and Kansas Envirothon's
content areas, and adds St. John's Catholic School (Beloit, KS) as
a verified state-to-international benchmark. This closes the Pillar 2
(Environmental Studies) evidence gap at the competition-benchmark level.

Confidence tags: revised standard (REVISION-2026-11-25 §2).

---

## 11.1 NCF-Envirothon — official competition structure

**Organizer:** National Conservation Foundation (NCF)
**Official page:** https://envirothon.org/
**Competition page:** https://envirothon.org/the-competition/
**Areas of Study:** https://envirothon.org/the-competition/areas-of-study/

**Eligibility (official):** High school students grades 9–12 (ages
14–19); teams of 5; 25,000+ students across US, Canada, China, and
Singapore [Moderate 2/2 — NCF official pages].

**Testing format.** Five hands-on/written station tests plus an Oral
Presentation where teams pitch a real-world environmental management
solution to a panel of judges [Moderate 2/2 — NCF official pages]:

| Station | Content area |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Soils & Land Use** — soil horizons, texture, structure, land capability, erosion, conservation practices |
| 2 | **Aquatic Ecology** — water quality, macroinvertebrate indicators, riparian buffers, watershed health |
| 3 | **Forestry** — tree identification, forest management, timber measurement, succession, ecology |
| 4 | **Wildlife** — habitat, wildlife management, species identification, population dynamics |
| 5 | **Current Issue** — a rotating annual topic (e.g. Rangeland Management, Water Resources, Sustainable Agriculture); hands-on test |
| Oral | **Oral Presentation** — teams present a management plan addressing the Current Issue to judges; scored /200 |

Scoring: each station test scored out of 100; Oral Presentation out
of 200; total possible 700 [Moderate 1/1 — NCF competition page].

**Test writing guidelines:** https://envirothon.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/NCFE-TestWritingGuideline.February2020.pdf
[Moderate 1/1 — official NCF document].

## 11.2 Kansas Envirothon — state-level content areas

**Organizer:** Kansas Association of Conservation Districts (KACD)
**Official page:** https://www.kacd.net/envirothon.cfm

**Content areas (Kansas-specific):** Kansas Envirothon explicitly tests
5 content areas [Moderate 1/1 — KACD official page]:

1. **Soils & Land Use**
2. **Forestry**
3. **Wildlife**
4. **Aquatic Ecology**
5. **Rangeland Management** (Kansas-specific; replaces the rotating
   Current Issue at the state level in some years)

**Progression:** Local → Area → State → NCF-Envirothon (international)
[Moderate 1/1 — KACD official page].

**Benchmark significance [Inferred]:** St. John's provides a verified
state-to-international progression: a small Catholic school in rural
Kansas competing at the national/international level against 50+
teams from across the US, Canada, China, and Singapore. This validates
the NCF-Envirothon as a meaningful competition ceiling for Meridian's
T3 Environmental Studies content. The progression from Kansas
Envirothon (state-specific 5 content areas) to NCF-Envirothon
(international 5 stations + Oral) is a natural difficulty ladder.
