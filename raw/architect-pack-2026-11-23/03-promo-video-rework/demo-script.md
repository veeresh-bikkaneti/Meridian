### 2. First-run tutorial invite (5–10s) — lower-third: none
- Invite banner appears ("Take the tour" / "Not now").

- "Next place" → drop on the exact spot → score 225, streak climbs.
- VO: "Nail it, and your score and streak climb."

- "Play the globe" → pin → reveal ("2,910 km" badge), globe dive.
- VO: "Or play the whole globe — every pin is a geography lesson."

### 6. State edition (40–46s) — lower-third: "State edition"

- VO: "Or drill down to a single state."

### 7. Misses-review deck (46–54s) — lower-third: "Review my misses"
- Picker banner "Review my misses" → Start review → one review card.
