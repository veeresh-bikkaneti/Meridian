## Description
Open friend challenges to all Meridian editions and all phases, beyond the current Globe-only end-of-game button. A design council delivered a signed manifest covering edition and region handling, radii, and challenge surfaces, with GeoDetective and Cold Trail excluded by default. Four packaging, scope, scoring, and privacy rulings from Veeresh are needed before building starts.
