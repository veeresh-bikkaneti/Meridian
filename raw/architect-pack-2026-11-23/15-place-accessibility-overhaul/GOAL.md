# Meridian place accessibility overhaul

Goal ID: goal_00b34d3dcd84
Goal slug: meridian-place-accessibility-overhaul

## Description
Veeresh's phone play-test found Cold Trail sighting anchors unusable for kids and the Place ring button auto-revealing answers instead of letting players place the ring. A Scrum-panel spec covers a real placement mode, a famous-anchor gazetteer, and age-banded place pools across editions. His separate AI builds it; Chitti reviews against the acceptance criteria, with his 'a kid could do this' play-test as the final gate.
