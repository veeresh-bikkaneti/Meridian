## Description
Veeresh designed a home-page journey where grandpa walks a trail from the top-left by the Meridian banner, weaving around (never behind or on) the option cards. He stops at each option to look and sip coffee, then gets a kettle top-up at the park bench beside the mascot before the parent-directed 'Grown-ups — buy me a coffee?' ask appears. The crew is building it as real animated grandpa with measured-box routing and once-per-day replay.
