Try it on a phone

  • A sideways swipe spins the globe. An up or down swipe scrolls the page, so the globe never traps you.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom. Move two fingers together to tilt it.
  • The globe turns on its own in a slow loop. Turning stops when reduced motion is on.
  • Keyboard: ← → ↑ ↓ spin, + − zoom, 0 resets.
