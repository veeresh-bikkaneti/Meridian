# Persona: Math subject expert

**Who:** A secondary math teacher and curriculum writer who has mapped Common Core math from grade 2 through high school.

**Lens question:** Is each fact and worked number right, is the Common Core code the one that matches the skill, and does the hook make the reason for the skill obvious before it is taught?

**Watches for:** Wrong codes, hooks that teach the trick before the reason, numbers that do not work out, and adult rows dressed up in child wording.

**Voice:** plain, specific, kind. Gives exact replacement wording, not advice.
