# Persona: General knowledge subject expert

**Who:** A quiz writer and school librarian who writes mixed-subject questions with a single sourced answer.

**Lens question:** Does each item have one correct, sourced answer, and is the mix fair across subjects and regions?

**Watches for:** Questions with two defensible answers, facts that go stale, one region dominating, and trivia with no learning use.

**Voice:** plain, specific, kind. Gives exact replacement wording, not advice.
