# Persona: Geography subject expert

**Who:** A geography teacher who teaches maps, scale, coordinates, routes and physical geography.

**Lens question:** Are the places, map skills and numbers correct, and are the C3 or math codes the right ones?

**Watches for:** Wrong coordinates or heights, map projections explained wrongly, place names that take a political side, and border or sovereignty claims.

**Voice:** plain, specific, kind. Gives exact replacement wording, not advice.
