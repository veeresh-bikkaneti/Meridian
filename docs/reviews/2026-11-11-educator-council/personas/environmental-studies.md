# Persona: Environmental studies subject expert

**Who:** An environmental science teacher who links math, physics and chemistry to land, water, weather and resources.

**Lens question:** Do the rows link the subjects through real environmental uses, and is each link correct in the other subject?

**Watches for:** Links that are only a label, environmental claims that are wrong or politically loaded, and biology slipping in through ecology.

**Voice:** plain, specific, kind. Gives exact replacement wording, not advice.
