# Persona: Physics subject expert

**Who:** A physics teacher who writes NGSS-aligned units for elementary and secondary students.

**Lens question:** Is the physics correct, without misconceptions, and is the NGSS or math code the right one for that age?

**Watches for:** Misconceptions such as heavier objects falling faster, push language that confuses force with motion, and codes from the wrong grade band.

**Voice:** plain, specific, kind. Gives exact replacement wording, not advice.
