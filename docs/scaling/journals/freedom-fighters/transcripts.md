## freedom-fighters:robben-island (group 10-18, stretch 18+)

**Opening question (read aloud, before play):** Nelson Mandela was brought to
Robben Island in 1964. He was moved away in 1982. How many years apart are
those dates? Solve 1964 + x = 1982. *(Answer: x = 18.)* [F1-1, F1-2, F1-3]

**Narration (18 spoken words, opener included, PROPOSED):**
> Hey kids, have you heard about this place? Nelson Mandela was held here.
> Today it is a museum. [F1-1, F1-6, F1-7]

**What the picture shows (for the transcript):** a ferry crossing the bay
towards a low, flat island [F1-8, F1-9]; the outside of the long, plain prison
buildings and the small lighthouse, in calm daylight [F1-10]; visitors walking
on the path [F1-6]. No cell interior, no guards, no fences in close-up.

**Retrieval check (read aloud, tap one):** What is the old prison on Robben
Island today?
- **A museum** (correct) [F1-6, F1-7]
- A school
- A hotel

Hint after a miss (one hint, then the review deck): "Listen again to the last
line of the movie."

**18+ stretch (after the retrieval check, contract I5 `stretch`):** Robben
Island is about 3.3 km long and 1.9 km wide. Treat it as an ellipse. About
what area is that? *(Answer: about 4.9 sq km, π × 1.65 × 0.95. The listed area
is 5.08 sq km.)* [F1-8]

**Note for parents and teachers (transcript only, not narrated):** Robben
Island has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999 [F1-5]. Its prison
held political prisoners; the last left in 1991 and the prison closed in 1996
[F1-7]. The clip does not tell why anyone was held there (standard §7a).
