# Robben Island screenplay

Clip `freedom-fighters:robben-island`. Ten seconds. 1152 by 768. 24 frames per second. No words painted on the picture. Nothing flashes.

The spoken lines are the caption and nothing else.

"Hey kids, have you heard about this place? Nelson Mandela was held here. Today it is a museum."

The card shows the opening question from `clips.json`. That question is not spoken in the film.

"Nelson Mandela was brought to Robben Island in 1964. He was moved away in 1982. How many years apart are those dates? Solve 1964 + x = 1982."

Sound has no bed. Where a beat has no narrator line, the sound is silence.

## Waiting, 0 to 2 seconds

The ferry sits on calm water and starts toward the low island.

Narrator words.

"Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"

## Build, 2 to 4 seconds

The ferry moves closer. The long plain buildings and the small lighthouse grow a little larger.

No new narrator sentence. The opener may still be finishing.

## Key moment, 4 to 6 seconds

The picture eases closer to the buildings. A gentle vignette sits on this beat only. Nothing flashes.

Narrator words.

"Nelson Mandela was held here."

## Reaction, 6 to 8 seconds

The visitors stay small on the path. The ferry slows.

Narrator words.

"Today it is a museum."

## Settle, 8 to 10 seconds

The picture holds. The water keeps a small movement.

No narrator words. Silence. No bed.
