## evac-school-morning

On 1 September 1939 the government began the evacuation. Children of Myrdle School in Stepney assembled at school at 5am that Friday.

- Source: UK government history blog, 30 Aug 2019. https://history.blog.gov.uk/2019/08/30/child-evacuees-in-the-second-world-war-operation-pied-piper-at-80/
- Confidence: HIGH
- Used by: `school-morning` narration (the date, the school, 5 in the morning)

## evac-counts-conflict

The same blog says the start moved over 1.5 million people from urban areas, of whom 800,000 were children. A BBC History page says nearly 3 million people were moved in the first four days.

- Sources: the blog above; https://www.bbc.com/history/british/britain_wwtwo/evacuees_01.shtml
- Confidence: HIGH that the pages disagree. Neither count is taught.

## quiet-hour-1918

The armistice was signed at 5:45 am on 11 November 1918 at Compiègne and took effect at 11:00 am, ending the war on the Western Front.

- Source: https://www.britannica.com/topic/Armistice-Day
- Confidence: HIGH
- Used by: `quiet-hour` narration (11 November 1918, 11 am, the war went quiet), and the `quiet-hour` 18+ stretch: from 5:45 am to 11:00 am is 5 h 15 min = 315 minutes. The 5:45 time is asked only in that stretch.
