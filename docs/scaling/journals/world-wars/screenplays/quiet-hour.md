# quiet-hour

Clip id `world-wars:quiet-hour`. Picture is trees around a forest clearing at Clairière de l'Armistice, Compiègne, France. No words in the picture. No stone, no flags, and no people. Spoken words are the caption, in this order, and nothing else.

The narration file is one take of that caption, from the start of the clip. The opening sentence is about the first two seconds. The second sentence follows in the same take and runs past the key moment, because that sentence is longer than two seconds. A beat with no new line does not add words.

## Waiting, 0 to 2 seconds

Morning light sits in the trees. The frame stays wide.

Narrator says "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"

Sound under the words is silence. No background bed.

## Build, 2 to 4 seconds

The frame moves into the clearing. The trees stay still.

Narrator says nothing.

Sound is silence. No background bed.

## Key moment, 4 to 6 seconds

The frame holds on the clearing.

Narrator says "On 11 November 1918, at 11 am, the war here went quiet."

Sound under the words is silence. No background bed.

## Reaction, 6 to 8 seconds

The frame eases back. Nothing new enters the picture.

Narrator says nothing.

Sound is silence. No background bed.

## Settle, 8 to 10 seconds

The frame rests on the trees.

Narrator says nothing.

Sound is silence. No background bed.
