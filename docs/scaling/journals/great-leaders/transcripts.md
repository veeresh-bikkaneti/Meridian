# Great leaders journal — draft transcripts (Stage A)

DRAFT. Every spoken line comes from a sourced fact in `sources.md` (fact ids
in brackets). Nothing here is rendered yet. Word counts were made by hand;
whether a line fits about 8 s of speech is TO CONFIRM when the narration is
rendered in Stage B; if it does not fit, cut words rather than speed up the
voice. The opener is the recipe's fixed line (`docs/journal-movies.md`). The
opening question and the retrieval check are read aloud from the player card,
not the video frame (the recipe keeps text off the frame).

## great-leaders:lincoln-memorial (group 7-10)

**Opening question (read aloud, before play):** Work on the Lincoln Memorial
began in 1914. It was dedicated in 1922. How many years did it take to build?
*(Answer: 1922 − 1914 = 8. This is a gap between two past years, so it does
not use the current year. Any "how many years ago" question added later must
compute from the current year at play time, never a literal year: owner call,
2026-11-10.)*
[L-2, L-3]

**Narration (20 spoken words, PROPOSED):**
> Hey kids, have you heard about this place? Washington, D.C., 1922.
> Thirty-six columns outside. Inside, Lincoln sits, carved in marble.
> [L-3, L-4, L-7, L-8, L-12]

**What the picture shows (for the transcript):** the white marble memorial at
the end of the National Mall, ringed by its columns; the camera moves through
the entrance to the central hall, where a 19-foot marble statue of Abraham
Lincoln, the 16th president, sits in a large chair. No crowds, signs or
inscriptions are shown. [L-1, L-4, L-6, L-7, L-8, L-12]

**Retrieval check (read aloud, tap one):** In the big statue, does Lincoln sit
or stand? Choices: *He sits* (correct) · *He stands* · *He rides a horse*.
Hint on a miss: "Look at the big chair in the middle hall."

**Stretch (10-18 only, optional I5 field, read aloud after the retrieval
check):** Each outside column is 44 feet tall. The seated statue is 19 feet
tall. Solve 19 + x = 44 to find how much taller a column is. *(Answer: x = 25,
so a column is 25 feet taller.)* [L-14]

**Screen notes for the producer:** no "I Have a Dream" steps, no crowd or
march, no inscriptions, no murals, nothing about the reason for the 36 columns.
See `sources.md` L-X1 to L-X5.

**Narration (20 spoken words; the check's limit is 22):**

> He made the greeting 'Jai Hind' famous. [B-1, B-12, B-3]

**What the picture shows:** the outside of Netaji Bhawan, his family home in
Kolkata (built by his father in 1909, a museum since 1961), then its rooms

planes or wartime scenes. [B-8, B-9, B-12]

**Retrieval check (read aloud, tap one):** What greeting did Netaji make famous?
Choices: *Jai Hind* (correct) · *Good morning* · *Thank you*. Hint on a miss:
"Listen again to the end of the clip."

**18+ stretch:** TO CONFIRM. The sourced army sizes measure different things
