# ENIAC

Picture. A basement room with long rows of tall machine panels and many small tubes. No people. No glow on the tubes. No modern phone. No military scene. No words on the picture.

The spoken lines are the caption cues. Nothing else is said.

## 0-2

What moves. The basement stays still. The panels are already in the room.

Narrator words. "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"

Sound. That spoken line only. No music bed.

## 2-4

What moves. The view travels slowly along one row of panels.

Narrator words. "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?" "Philadelphia, 1946."

Sound. Those spoken lines only. No music bed.

## 4-6

What moves. The row fills more of the frame. The small tubes stay dull.

Narrator words. "Philadelphia, 1946." "ENIAC, an early computer, filled a big room."

Sound. Those spoken lines only. No music bed.

## 6-8

What moves. The view reaches the length of the row, so the room reads as large. This is the key moment. The tubes do not light up, and nothing flashes.

Narrator words. "ENIAC, an early computer, filled a big room."

Sound. That spoken line only. No music bed.

## 8-10

What moves. The view settles wide on the panels and the empty floor between them.

Narrator words. "ENIAC, an early computer, filled a big room." The line ends before the last half second, then the picture is quiet.

Sound. That spoken line, then silence. No music bed.
