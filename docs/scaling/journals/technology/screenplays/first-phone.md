# First phone

Picture. An attic hall in Boston, one wire along the hall, a wave moving on that wire, and one listener at a receiver at the far end. No words on the picture. No second machine.

The spoken lines are the caption cues. Nothing else is said.

## 0-2

What moves. The hall stays still. The wire is already there.

Narrator words. "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"

Sound. That spoken line only. No music bed.

## 2-4

What moves. The view drifts closer to the wire.

Narrator words. "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?" "Boston, 1876."

Sound. Those spoken lines only. No music bed.

## 4-6

What moves. A wave starts along the wire, toward the far end.

Narrator words. "Boston, 1876." "A voice travelled down a wire."

Sound. Those spoken lines only. No music bed.

## 6-8

What moves. The wave reaches the listener. The listener holds the receiver. This is the key moment, so the move is a little larger, and it does not flash.

Narrator words. "A voice travelled down a wire."

Sound. That spoken line only. No music bed.

## 8-10

What moves. The view settles on the listener and the wire. The wave rests.

Narrator words. "A voice travelled down a wire." The line ends before the last half second, then the picture is quiet.

Sound. That spoken line, then silence. No music bed.
