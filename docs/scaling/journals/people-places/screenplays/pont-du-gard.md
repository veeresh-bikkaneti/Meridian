# Pont du Gard

Ten seconds. The still is the three rows of arches over the river. No words on the picture.

## 0 to 2

**Moves.** The whole bridge sits in the frame above the water.

**Narrator.** "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"

**Sound.** No bed. Voice only, if the voice file is present.

## 2 to 4

**Moves.** A slow rise toward the top row of small arches.

**Narrator.** No new words.

**Sound.** No bed.

## 4 to 6

**Moves.** The top row is the closest thing in the frame. This is the key moment.

**Narrator.** "This bridge in France has 35 arches on its top row."

**Sound.** No bed. A small lift in the voice around this beat, then back.

## 6 to 8

**Moves.** The rise eases. The three rows stay stacked.

**Narrator.** No new words.

**Sound.** No bed.

## 8 to 10

**Moves.** The picture settles back to the whole bridge and the river.

**Narrator.** No new words.

**Sound.** No bed. The voice is done.
