## Headline goal (standard §0)

Show how living things use the physics and chemistry the child already learned,
and how they shape the environment. Every unit has a bridge from chemistry or
physics and a playable real-life application.

## Proposed units (structure only — no fact asserted until sourced)

Candidate ideas are the ones the earlier science lens proposed and set aside
under the old rule (Plant Lab, Food Web Weaver, Animal Homes, Body Quest, Cell
Explorer), reshaped to the progression map.

| Step | Unit | Builds on | Real life | Environment link | Playable scene idea (three.js; Flat = 2D) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Tiny building blocks of life — cells (Cell Explorer) | C-unit "tiny building blocks" | Looking through a microscope | Microbes in soil and water | Zoom from a leaf to its cells |
| B2 | Plants make food from light (Plant Lab) | P4 light, C1 states of matter | Gardens, farms | Forests and oxygen | Move a plant toward or away from light, predict growth |
| B3 | Water in living things | C2 evaporation, C3 dissolving | Watering plants, drinking water | Rivers, wetlands, the water cycle through plants | Follow water from roots to leaves to clouds |
| B4 | Body systems at work (Body Quest — movement, breathing, heart, digestion only) | P1 forces, P2 levers, C3 dissolving | Sport, exercise, eating | Clean air and water for healthy bodies | Bones and muscles as levers; lift and predict |
| B5 | Food webs and energy (Food Web Weaver) | P3 energy, M2 fractions | What we eat | Ecosystems on the globe | Build a food web; remove one link and predict what changes |
| B6 | Habitats and adaptations (Animal Homes) | B5, map skills | Pets, zoos, parks | Deserts, oceans, rainforests, poles | Fly the globe to a habitat; match animal features to it |
| B7 | Decomposers and recycling in nature | C4 reactions, B5 | Compost | Soil and nutrient cycles | Watch a leaf break down over seasons |
| E | Environmental studies capstone | B5–B7 + math, physics, chemistry | A garden, a park plan | One real place read through every subject | Pick a place on the globe and keep its ecosystem balanced |

**Out of scope:** human reproduction, sexuality, sexual orientation and gender
identity (owner ruling); dissection or graphic imagery; medical advice; any
contested topic per §7a.

## Standards

NGSS life-science performance expectations (LS1 structures and processes, LS2
ecosystems) for grades 5 through high school, each code verified with a live
source or marked UNVERIFIED. Disciplinary core ideas on reproduction, heredity
and sexual development are excluded from this spec.
