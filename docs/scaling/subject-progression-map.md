# Subject progression map — math → physics → chemistry → biology, tied together by environmental studies

**Status:** PROPOSED structure, not content. No fact is asserted here; every unit
still needs sourced facts, standard codes and educator sign-off per
`k12-learning-design-standard.md`.

Owner, 2026-11-10: "each topic is related like math->physics->chem->biology and
their application in real life. and these topic are related to environmental
studies."

## The headline goal

Teach how the subjects connect and how they are applied (owner, 2026-11-10).
Every unit's first objective is the bridge and the application below; the
subject skill comes second (standard §0).

## The idea

Subjects are not separate games. Each unit builds on a skill the child already
earned in the subject before it, shows where that skill is used in real life,
and links to the environment around them. The globe and map (the game's home)
are where the environmental thread lives: every unit can end on a real place
where the idea matters.

```
MATH ──► PHYSICS ──► CHEMISTRY ──► BIOLOGY     
  │          │            │                 │
  └──────────┴─── ENVIRONMENTAL STUDIES ────┘   (the thread, shown on the globe)
```

## Every unit carries four fields

| Field | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Builds on** | The skill from the earlier subject (or earlier unit) the child needs first. The game checks it from the mastery record before unlocking; if missing, it offers a short refresher from the review deck instead of blocking. |
| **New skill** | The one observable objective (standard §2). |
| **Real life** | One everyday place the skill is used (kitchen, playground, road, sport, weather, building, travel). |
| **Environment link** | One way the idea shows up in nature or in caring for the planet, told as facts, not campaigns (content rule §7a: no contested policy). Ends on a real place on the globe where possible. |

## How it plays

- **Unlock by skill, not by age.** A unit opens when its "builds on" skills are
  Mastered or Growing; adults can jump ahead with a short check.
- **Bridges.** The last round of each unit previews the next subject ("You used
  fractions to share pizza. Now use them to mix colours.").
- **Globe as the thread.** Every unit's wrap-up can fly to one real place
  (existing three.js globe, dynamic import, Flat tier = 2D map pin) where the
  idea shows up in nature.
- **Review deck mixes subjects.** Misses from math come back inside physics
  rounds where the skill is used, so practice stays connected.
- **General knowledge** becomes the mixed review across the whole chain.

## What each spec adds

The math, physics, chemistry, biology, science, P4 and social-studies specs each add a
"Builds on / Real life / Environment link" column to their item list, and a
pointer to this map. Branch prompts are updated accordingly.
