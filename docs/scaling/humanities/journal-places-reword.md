# Journal places, told as architecture, geography and math only

**Status:** PROPOSED, 2026-11-10. GATED: no named human educator has signed
`educator-gate-rubric.md`. Owner decision 5 (2026-11-10, logged in
`humanities-definitions-spec.md`, "Owner decisions (2026-11-10)"): keep the
live journal places, and reword Angkor, Potala, Timbuktu and Uluru so they
talk only about the place: architecture, size, age, materials, geography,
height, distance, and the math you can do with them. No religion, belief,
ritual, sacred status or conflict is named.

**This file is the wording only.** The live text sits in files this branch
may not edit. The integrator applies it (see "Files the integrator must
change" below). Nothing here changes the live game.

**Owner rule (2026-11-10), verbatim:** "sometime history can be controversial
too, we just have to stirke the balance." [sic] Keep the place, teach only
settled facts (when built, how big, what from), and leave out anything still
argued over.

Later owner decisions applied here (same log): 7, Potala KEEP, architecture
only; 8, Alamo mission KEEP, architecture only; 9, reword Sagrada Família,
Fushimi Inari, Christ the Redeemer and Robber's Cave as architecture,
geography and math only; 10, four new PROPOSED places for balance (part B);
14-18 (t334u, 2026-11-10), sources for the Redeemer 30 m / 28 m hook and
for Potala's 13 storeys, Konark wheel hook with no part count, Uluru story
cut to its first sentence, Alamo hook confirmed as currentYear − 1718.

## Rules applied to every line

- Only place facts: what it is made of, how big, how high, how old, where.
- No words for religion, belief, ritual, worship, pilgrims, sacred status,
  holy places, rulers' religious roles, or any war, attack or dispute.
  In descriptions, building types that name a faith (temple, mosque,
  monastery, shrine) are replaced by plain words (stone complex, mud-brick buildings, palace).
- Every number has a row in the source table, or it is marked UNVERIFIED and
  is not used in a line a child hears or reads.
- Years-ago answers are worked out at play time as `currentYear − year`;
  no file stores today's year.
- Opening line stays the owner's: "Hey kids, have you heard about this place?"
- A proper name stays as it is, even when it contains a word like "Temple"
  or "Mission"; only the description around it uses plain words. Names that
  are a problem in themselves are flagged to the owner (see "Proper names
  flagged"), never renamed here.
- A number marked UNVERIFIED is never narrated or used in a question.
- Age group follows the grade of the math skill (standard §1: 7-10 = grades
  2-5, 10-18 = grades 5-12, 18+ = adults). No algebra or trigonometry in
  7-10. Groups are PROPOSED; the educator confirms each one.
- Model numbers (a walking speed, a ship's depth, a box shape) are labelled
  as models in the question.
