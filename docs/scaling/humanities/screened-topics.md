## How to read the columns

- **Group**: by school grade of the cited standard. 7-10 = grades 2-5, 10-18 = grades 5-12, 18+ = adults beyond grade 12. Grade 5 may go in either. No algebra or trigonometry in 7-10.
- **C3 family**: the C3 Framework dimension and grade band the topic would be coded under. **Every indicator number is UNVERIFIED** (see "Sources" below); the family is a pointer for the educator, not a citation. `D2.His` = History, `D2.Geo` = Geography, `D2.Civ` = Civics, `D2.Eco` = Economics.
- **Objective**: written the §2 way, with an observable verb and what the game sees. Counts of items per topic are not set (TO CONFIRM).

## Confirmed removals: religion and mythology

Checked on this branch, 2026-11-10:

- No topic in the list above teaches religion, religious practice, pilgrimage, scripture or mythology. H19 and H20 are cut.
- In `humanities-definitions-spec.md`, religion and mythology appear only as a **removal record**: the struck 2026-11-07 list (§2.2) and the coverage audit (§2.8). They are not planned content. They stay as history of the decision.
- **Finding for the integrator (not fixed here, outside my files):** the live 106 journal entries already include places that are also religious sites (the coverage audit names Sagrada Família, Fushimi Inari, Potala, Christ the Redeemer and the Alamo as a former mission) and two with a legend or sacred-site note (Uluru, Robber's Cave). Whether to retell those entries without the religious angle is a decision for the journal branches and the owner; this list only says no *new* religious or mythological stories are planned.
