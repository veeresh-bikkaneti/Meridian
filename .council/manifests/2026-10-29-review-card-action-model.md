## 1 — The owner's charge

Owner report 2026-10-29 (screenshot): on the home "Review my misses" card — *"button placement is messy, card itself is a button, i mean you can click anywhere on the button and we have 3 different buttons."* The card is a whole-card click target (Form S) for "Start review"; three separately shipped additions live inside it: the decorative REVIEW → cue pill, the "Start over" text link (the destructive history reset, PR #281), and the "Challenge a friend — pick a map" text link (PR #285). Each was individually panel-approved; the composition was never designed. The squads must answer with evidence: (1) should the card remain a whole-card button; (2) where do the three actions live so each has ONE clear, distinct affordance; (3) the cue's job; (4) the accessibility contract.

## 2 — The decided model (Chair synthesis, after one adversarial round)

The Review card **leaves Form S** and becomes a panel with three explicit controls, in DOM = visual = tab order:

1. **Start review — filled primary pill** (`.hv5-btn-brown` treatment with the card-surface fallback-var pattern), visible text "Start review", existing accessible name "Review my misses: Start review" **unchanged**. Rendered only when misses are due. Form S is otherwise untouched.
2. **Challenge a friend — pick a map — outline pill** (2px border, no underline, NOT filled), same treatment in due AND caught-up states (one control, one look). Present iff `friendChallenges` is on AND deck total > 0 (existing gate).
3. **Start over — last, in a hairline-separated danger footer** (the divider belongs to the footer; see §5 F7). Fixed-hex danger colour **`#b3372a`** (theme-invariant; 5.80:1 on the card) — NOT `var(--atlas-stamp)`, whose night-theme value fails the text floor (see §5 F2). Underline retained; ≥44px target incl. padding; scoped focus outline in the same fixed red. `ResetHistoryControl`, its dialog, its copy, and its ReviewComplete rendering are **unchanged**; the override is Review-card-scoped only.
4. **Caught-up state:** status text → Challenge (same outline pill) → Start-over footer. No primary.
5. **Empty state:** unchanged (decorative card, no controls).
6. **Flag off:** Challenge absent; no empty stack slot (the footer's divider is not an orphan — it is the footer's own).

**The cue is REMOVED** (span, `.hv5-review-cue` rules, its forced-colors restatement) — not demoted. Recorded brand rule: *"Stretch applies only to single-intent cards. Multi-intent cards use explicit controls."*

**Defect framing (binding):** overlapping/contained hit areas arbitrated by z-index — not DOM nesting, not obstruction. Form-S hit-testing depends on z-index across the whole stylesheet; the fix removes the mechanism for this card (no survival clause: the CTA loses `.hv5-stretch` and no `::after` may cover it).

**Accessibility floors (binding):** targets ≥44×44px measured border-box; inter-target gaps ≥8px; controls inset ≥14px from the card edge; focus order = DOM order; every focus ring ≥3:1 against every adjacent surface **including art pixels** (pixel-sampled at verify, not an E2E assertion); the dark-backdrop ring uses cream/brass `#e8b64c` (8.74:1), never `#77591f` on the night backdrop (2.52:1 — Accessibility Auditor veto, Phase 1); forced-colors distinctions are structural (border, divider, position), never colour-only.
