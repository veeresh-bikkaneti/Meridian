# Source: Why Learn adoption research pack (2026-11-24)

Summary of the reverse-engineering and adoption-design research for
Meridian's Why Learn page. The immutable pack lives in
`raw/why-learn-adoption/`; this page is its wiki summary.

## What the pack contains

- The RE pack: measured interaction patterns from Lesson2Play and
  RoverKit (fully measured) and Every Joule (partially — its live 3D
  book needs GPU WebGL and did not render on the research machine;
  its mechanics were recovered from its shipped code), with 17 named
  patterns P1-P17
  (source: raw/why-learn-adoption/RE-PACK.md).
- The adoption brief: two brainstorm rounds (the second on the
  owner's corrected scope — Lesson2Play + Every Joule only, RoverKit
  parked as a future robotic direction, no literal copy-paste of
  source topics), lane verdicts, and the band ruling (keep current
  bands, target within them)
  (source: raw/why-learn-adoption/why-learn-adoption-brief.md).
- Four lane specs: stamps, the Predict pilot, the sticker-skin
  visuals, and the scene hinge — including the owner's addenda that
  the explanation is a motion video with per-band cuts and the
  question is interactable
  (source: raw/why-learn-adoption/specs/lane-b-predict.md).
- Capture evidence (videos and stills; extracted frame sequences
  were left out as regenerable intermediates — the source videos
  are included)
  (source: raw/why-learn-adoption/captures).

## Where the work stands

The staged mock and the interactable prototype built from these
specs live on the `mock/why-learn-adoption` branch. Production
decisions still open with the owner are recorded in the brief
(source: raw/why-learn-adoption/why-learn-adoption-brief.md).
