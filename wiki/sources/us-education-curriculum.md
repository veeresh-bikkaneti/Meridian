# Source: U.S. education curriculum research pack (2026-11-24)

Summary of the dual-cohort curriculum research commissioned for
Meridian's Why Learn page. The immutable pack lives in
`raw/us-education-curriculum/`; this page is its wiki summary. The
design document is
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md) and its
deduplicated source index is
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/SOURCES.md).

## What the pack contains

- A district sample justification table covering Cohort 1 (NYCPS,
  LAUSD, Chicago, Miami-Dade, Houston ISD plus national/state
  standards) and Cohort 2, six districts selected from official ESSA
  state report-card portals with verified ratings and growth metrics
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#0-district-sample-justification-table--who-the-design-is-calibrated-against).
- A recommended four-tier structure for ages 7 and up, with the
  difficulty dial defined as quantification level
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a-recommended-tier-structure-inferred-design-on-the-cited-evidence).
- A curriculum matrix mapping standards to Meridian's 6 categories
  across the 4 tiers
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#b-concise-curriculum-matrix--standards--meridians-6-categories--4-tiers).
- 96 case-file prompts, 4 per subject per tier
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#c-case-file-prompts--why-do-we-learn-this).
- Gap and priority recommendations measured against Meridian's
  inventory of 131 items counted from the code on main
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane4-meridian-inventory.md).

## Headline findings

- Math holds 53 of the 131 items; General Knowledge holds 10 and
  Social Studies 12, the thinnest categories relative to U.S. K-12
  curriculum weight
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane4-meridian-inventory.md).
- Life Science, one of the four NGSS discipline families, has zero
  items in the inventory; the pack records the open owner decision
  between a 7th category and a named strand inside General Knowledge
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a6-two-structural-rulings-the-tiers-depend-on).
- The four design tiers collapse onto Meridian's current 3 age bands
  with no re-authoring
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a5-collapse-to-3-buttons-ui-constraint-note-inferred).

## Revision 2026-11-25 — Living World strand + confidence standard

- The Life Science question is decided. Biology/Life Science is a
  protected first-class strand named Living World inside General
  Knowledge, with internal id `life-science`. The six-category UI
  stays
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#1-decision-status--adopted).
- At least one-third of General Knowledge T1–T2 allocation is the
  planning target for primary Living World items. The target is a
  product-balancing rule, not a district instructional-time claim
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#1-decision-status--adopted).
- The inventory audit classified 78 items across General Knowledge,
  Journals, Chemistry, Physics, and Social Studies. It found 0
  primary Living World items, 0 cross-tagged items, and 0 of the 8
  Living World domains represented, so every Living World item must
  be authored new
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#3-inventory-audit-tested).
- The confidence standard was replaced. High now requires at least
  two independent primary or official sources, with a narrow-fact
  exception for a single authoritative source
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#2-confidence-standard--replaced).
- The confidence audit counted 475 tags and ruled 10 corrections.
  The largest are [High 2/3] to [Moderate 2/3] for the Eanes ISD
  scaled score and growth claims, because both supporters share one
  origin, and [High 3/4] to [Moderate 3/4] for the California
  adoption-dates claim
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#7-confidence-audit).
- Cohort 2 is now described as six districts selected from official
  state accountability portals for high performance plus documented
  growth, change, or growth-related evidence. A proficiency trend or
  accountability-model component is not a direct student-growth
  result
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#7-confidence-audit).
- Meridian currently covers selected Physical Science and Earth &
  Space content, with Life Science assigned to a protected General
  Knowledge strand. Full NGSS science coverage is not yet
  established
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#9-ngss-coverage-statement).
- Lane 6 anchors the strand on official NGSS performance
  expectations. LS1 and LS2 carry records across T1 to T3. LS3 and
  LS4 are anchored at T1 only, and no national T4 standard exists
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane6-life-science.md).

## Revision 2026-11-26 - competition-anchored rebuild of the five pillars

- The plan is rebuilt on the branch's 4 tiers against verified
  top-rated districts and national competition standards, in five
  pillars (STEM, Environmental Studies, Traffic & Road Rules,
  Situational Awareness, Educational Riddles)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-competition-anchored-rebuild.md#4-the-five-pillars-x-tiers-x-national-competition-benchmarks).
- Every gap in the external audit of 2026-11-26 is mapped to a
  closing lane: A1 pacing pass, A2 olympiads, A3 young scientist,
  A4 environmental competitions, A5 safety/situational/riddles, and
  the P1-P5 pillar gaps
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-competition-anchored-rebuild.md#1-gaps-from-the-audit-and-where-each-is-fixed).
- Competition benchmarks with official eligibility rules: AMC 8/10/12
  (MAA, grades 8/10/12 and below), MATHCOUNTS (grades 6-8), Science
  Olympiad Divisions A/B/C (through Gr 6 / Gr 6-9 / Gr 9-12), USAPhO,
  USNCO, USABO (Gr 9-12)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane7-competitions-olympiads.md).
- Young Scientist and environmental benchmarks: 3M Young Scientist
  Challenge (Gr 5-8), Thermo Fisher JIC (Gr 6-8), Regeneron ISEF
  (Gr 9-12), NCF-Envirothon (Gr 9-12), National Ocean Sciences Bowl
  (high school)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane8-young-scientist-environmental.md).
- Traffic/safety sources: NHTSA Child Pedestrian Safety Curriculum
  K-5, Bicycle Safer Journey, Teen Driving/GDL, FHWA MUTCD 11th
  Edition, state DMV handbooks
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane9-safety-situational-logic.md#91-traffic--road-rules-pillar-3--verified-official-sources).
- District pacing-guide pass closes Lane 5's open cells: St. Johns
  HS course sequences via SPP + Year-at-a-Glance + 2025-26 catalogs,
  Chandler per-grade scope-and-sequence pages, Triad grade-level
  pages, plus the federal NCES CCD identity layer
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane10-district-pacing-pass.md#103-pacing-guide--course-sequence-closures-this-pass).
- Depth rule adopted: each pillar cell carries a base anchor
  (standard + district sequence) and a competition ceiling anchor per
  tier (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-competition-anchored-rebuild.md#5-depth-re-calibration-rule-adopted).

## Revision 2026-11-26 — Biology promoted to 7th subject card

- Biology is promoted from the Living World strand inside General
  Knowledge to its own standalone 7th subject card (Option A approved,
  superseding the interim strand decision of 2026-11-25)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-biology-7th-card.md#1-decision--option-a-approved).
- Biology subject module created at `src/subjects/biology/` with 14
  records: 4 T1 (7-10), 5 T2 (10-18 Middle School), 4 T3 (10-18
  High School), 1 T4 (18+). All anchored on NGSS Life Science PEs
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-biology-7th-card.md#3-implementation-completed).
- USABO confirmed as the T3 competition ceiling for Biology
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane6-pass2-heredity-evolution.md).

## Revision 2026-11-26 — Lane 6 Pass 2 (LS3/LS4 at T2/T3)
