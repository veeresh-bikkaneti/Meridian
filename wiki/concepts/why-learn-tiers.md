# Concept: Why Learn tier structure

The curriculum design for Why Learn is calibrated to four tiers cut
on grade boundaries, because the frameworks themselves break there
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a2-why-these-boundaries--the-evidence):

- **T1 Upper Elementary** — ages 7-10, Grades 2-5, split internally
  at Grade 2 (T1a) vs Grades 3-5 (T1b)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a4-the-grade-2-straddle--internal-split-of-t1-inferred-council-t1-seat-10).
- **T2 Middle School** — ages 11-14, Grades 6-8.
- **T3 High School** — ages 14-18, Grades 9-12.
- **T4 College & Adult** — ages 18+, designed as a small
  application-first layer because no national standard covers it
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a1-the-four-tiers).

The difficulty dial between tiers is quantification: qualitative at
T1, relational at T2, formal at T3
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a3-the-difficulty-dial--the-councils-central-calibration-finding).

Meridian's shipping age bands stay 7-10 / 10-18 / 18+ per the owner's
standing ruling; the tiers fold onto them with the T2/T3 split kept
as an internal difficulty tag
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a5-collapse-to-3-buttons-ui-constraint-note-inferred).

## Biology as the 7th subject card (2026-11-26)

Biology is a standalone subject card with its own picker entry, icon
(Leaf), and one-liner. The interim Living World strand inside General
Knowledge (adopted 2026-11-25) is superseded. Biology content is
anchored on NGSS Life Science DCIs (LS1-LS4) across T1-T3, with
USABO as the T3 competition ceiling
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-biology-7th-card.md).

Lane 6 Pass 2 closes the LS3/LS4 gap: Heredity and Evolution are now
fully anchored at T2 (MS-LS3-1, MS-LS3-2, MS-LS4-1, MS-LS4-2,
MS-LS4-4, MS-LS4-6) and T3 (HS-LS3-1, HS-LS3-2, HS-LS3-3, HS-LS4-1,
HS-LS4-2, HS-LS4-3, HS-LS4-4, HS-LS4-5), with state-standard
corroboration from TX, FL, and CA
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane6-pass2-heredity-evolution.md).

## Five-pillar competition anchors per tier (2026-11-26)

Each pillar cell now carries a competition ceiling anchor beside its
standards base anchor
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-competition-anchored-rebuild.md#5-depth-re-calibration-rule-adopted):

- **STEM:** T2 ceiling = AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS, Science Olympiad Div B, 3M
  Young Scientist, Thermo Fisher JIC; T3 ceiling = AMC 10/12, SciOly
  Div C, USAPhO/USNCO/USABO, Regeneron ISEF
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-competition-anchored-rebuild.md#pillar-1--stem-math--science-olympiad--young-scientist-depth).
- **Environmental Studies:** T3 ceiling = NCF-Envirothon (5 field
  stations + Oral Presentation) and National Ocean Sciences Bowl (8
  disciplines); Kansas Envirothon tests 5 core areas (Soils & Land Use,
  Forestry, Wildlife, Aquatic Ecology, Rangeland Management) PLUS the
  annual Current Environmental Issue and Oral Presentation; St. John's
  Catholic School (Beloit, KS) verified as state-to-international
  benchmark (`2024–2026` 3× Kansas champion, `2024` Geneva NY, `2025`
  Calgary `24th/51`, `2026` Mississippi State Team #2640 `499.00`;
  `"12th place"` cited to KACD past winners, `[Moderate]`)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-26-review-pushback-fixes.md#3-kansas-envirothon--2026-ncf-envirothon-corrections-lane11).
- **Traffic & Road Rules:** T1 = NHTSA K-5 pedestrian curriculum; T2 =
  Bicycle Safer Journey + MUTCD signs; T3 = state DMV handbooks +
  NHTSA GDL + MUTCD; T4 = adult driving decisions
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane9-safety-situational-logic.md#91-traffic--road-rules-pillar-3--verified-official-sources).
- **Situational Awareness:** sourced NHTSA/ESS3 base at T1-T3 plus an
  [Inferred] scan-assess-decide-act protocol layer at T4
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane9-safety-situational-logic.md#92-situational-awareness-pillar-4--source-base--honest-scope).
- **Educational Riddles:** T2 = MATHCOUNTS/AMC 8 logic; T3 = AMC 10/12
  + SciOly deduction events + NOSB team challenges
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/evidence/lane9-safety-situational-logic.md#93-educational-riddles-pillar-5--competition-grade-logic-benchmarks).
