# Concept: Why Learn tier structure

The curriculum design for Why Learn is calibrated to four tiers cut
on grade boundaries, because the frameworks themselves break there
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a2-why-these-boundaries--the-evidence):

- **T1 Upper Elementary** — ages 7-10, Grades 2-5, split internally
  at Grade 2 (T1a) vs Grades 3-5 (T1b)
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a4-the-grade-2-straddle--internal-split-of-t1-inferred-council-t1-seat-10).
- **T2 Middle School** — ages 11-14, Grades 6-8.
- **T3 High School** — ages 14-18, Grades 9-12.
- **T4 College & Adult** — ages 18+, designed as a small
  application-first layer because no national standard covers it
  (source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a1-the-four-tiers).

The difficulty dial between tiers is quantification: qualitative at
T1, relational at T2, formal at T3
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a3-the-difficulty-dial--the-councils-central-calibration-finding).

Meridian's shipping age bands stay 7-10 / 10-18 / 18+ per the owner's
standing ruling; the tiers fold onto them with the T2/T3 split kept
as an internal difficulty tag
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/CURRICULUM-DESIGN.md#a5-collapse-to-3-buttons-ui-constraint-note-inferred).

## Living World inside the tiers (2026-11-25)

Life Science sits inside General Knowledge as the protected Living
World strand in every tier. T1 treatment is observable phenomena
only. T2 is integrated and relational. T3 is formal Biology. T4 is
application-oriented biological literacy with no medical advice
(source: raw/us-education-curriculum/REVISION-2026-11-25-living-world.md#5-revised-matrix--life-science-strand-treatment).
